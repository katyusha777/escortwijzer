## Researched specifics (fetch-verified 2026-08-01)

1. **Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE) 2026:** 21–25 October 2026 (5 days), 31st edition, ~1,000+ events across 200+ venues, huge international influx.
   Source: https://www.amsterdam-dance-event.nl/en/ + iamsterdam.com
2. **F1 Dutch Grand Prix (Zandvoort) 2026:** 21–23 August 2026, and 2026 is the LAST contracted year — the organiser states "this will be the very last time" F1 races on Dutch soil; contract with FOM ends after 2026. 2026 adds an F1 Sprint. Zandvoort is ~30 km / ~30 min by train from Amsterdam Centraal; many attendees stay in Amsterdam.
   Source: https://dutchgp.com/en/date-f1-dutch-gp-2026/
3. **Koningsdag:** 27 April every year (King Willem-Alexander's birthday); Mon 27 April 2026, Tue 27 April 2027. If 27 April is a Sunday it moves to the 26th (not the case 2026/27). City-wide street party, canals jammed.
   Source: https://publicholidays.nl/kings-day/ + rijksoverheid
4. **Pride / WorldPride Amsterdam 2026:** WorldPride Amsterdam runs 25 July – 8 August 2026 (Amsterdam hosts WorldPride 2026 — much bigger than the annual Pride); Canal Parade Sat 1 August 2026, 12:00–18:00.
   Source: https://pride.amsterdam/en/worldpride/ + iamsterdam.com
5. **IBC (International Broadcasting Convention):** 11–14 September 2026 at RAI Amsterdam, ~44,000 visitors from 170 countries — the big autumn business-conference spike (hotels fill, rates jump).
   Source: https://show.ibc.org/
6. **Demand/price mechanics (keep consistent with price guide):** Amsterdam independents €200–€350/hr; outcall surcharge €30–€80; agencies from ~€250/hr. During peak events availability tightens and rates firm at the top of the band — no invented % figures; frame honestly. Hotels fill first, so outcall logistics get harder (traffic, hotel visitor checks busier).
   Source: our /en/guides/escort-prices-netherlands/
7. **Safety/scam angle:** high-demand weekends = more fake ads, more too-cheap bait, more "book now, pay deposit" pressure aimed at visitors who can't shop around. Link spot-fakes. Cash-in-person norm unchanged; any pre-meeting voucher/crypto/giftcard payment = scam (Fraudehelpdesk).

## The opinion
Book before the event weekend, not during it. The professionals worth seeing are booked out early on ADE/F1/Koningsdag weekends, and the thin late supply is exactly where the event-timed scam ads sit. On a normal Tuesday you can afford to browse; on Canal Parade Saturday you cannot.

## The flaw (in what we recommend)
No public dataset proves the size of the event spike — the "availability tightens, rates firm up" claim is reasoned market observation, not a measured number, and we say so. And booking ahead means committing before you know your own plans on a chaotic weekend.
