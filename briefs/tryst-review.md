## Opinion
Tryst proves verification and low advertiser cost can coexist — a sex-worker-run company checks every profile including the free tier, which the big Dutch platforms claim is unworkable. But the model was built for the Anglosphere, and the Dutch section is an afterthought: ~112 female profiles, effectively Amsterdam-only, priced in dollars.

## Flaw in every recommendation
- Tryst itself: tiny NL inventory (Amsterdam-centric), EN-only UI, USD-first rates, support on Australian office hours.
