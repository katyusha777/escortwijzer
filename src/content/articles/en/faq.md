## Is it legal to book an escort in the Netherlands?

Yes. Sex work between consenting adults has been legal since the brothel ban was lifted on 1 October 2000, and paying for sex is not a crime for the client — that applies to tourists exactly as it does to locals.

→ Go deeper: [is escort legal in the Netherlands?](/en/law/is-escort-legal-netherlands/)

## How much does an escort cost in the Netherlands?

Expect €150 to €250 per hour for an independent escort and €250 to €400 or more through an agency or in the high-class segment. Anything well under €100 per hour is almost always fake.

→ Go deeper: [escort prices in the Netherlands](/en/guides/escort-prices-netherlands/)

## Is an escort more expensive in Amsterdam?

Yes: budget €200 to €350 per hour in Amsterdam, roughly €50 to €100 above Rotterdam, The Hague or Utrecht. Tourism and hotel outcalls drive the premium.

→ Go deeper: [escorts in Amsterdam](/en/cities/escort-amsterdam/)

## How much does an overnight with an escort cost?

An overnight of 8 to 12 hours typically runs €800 to €1,200 with independents and €1,500 to €2,500 in the high-class segment. Per hour that is far cheaper than single hours.

→ Go deeper: [escort prices in the Netherlands](/en/guides/escort-prices-netherlands/)

## Do I have to pay an escort in advance?

No, never remotely before the appointment. Anyone demanding upfront payment via paysafecard, gift cards or crypto is by definition a scammer.

→ Go deeper: [spotting fake escort profiles](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/)

## How do you pay an escort in the Netherlands?

Cash, at the start of the appointment, ideally in an envelope; some escorts also take a Dutch bank payment (Tikkie) or card on the spot. After that, money is off the table.

## Can I get in legal trouble as a client?

Only if the sex worker is under 18 (Articles 245 and 246 of the Dutch Criminal Code, up to nine years — even if you did not know the age) or coerced. Check age and consent and your legal risk is close to zero.

→ Go deeper: [legal age and client risk](/en/law/escort-legal-age-client-risk/)

## How old do escorts have to be in the Netherlands?

18 nationally, but many cities including Amsterdam, Rotterdam and The Hague require 21 at licensed businesses. On 13 May 2026 the cabinet announced plans to raise the national minimum to 21.

→ Go deeper: [legal age and client risk](/en/law/escort-legal-age-client-risk/)

## How do you book an escort?

Pick a verified profile, send a short polite message with date, duration and location, answer the screening questions and confirm the rate in advance. Explicit language in a first message is considered rude.

## Can an escort come to my hotel?

Usually yes: most Dutch hotels allow registered guests to receive visitors, and hotel outcalls are the standard visitor booking. Expect the escort to ask your hotel name, room number and booking name — that screening is normal and mutual.

## What is the difference between an escort and the red light windows?

An escort is booked in advance and meets you at a private address or your hotel; at windows such as De Wallen you agree everything on the spot at a licensed window brothel. Both are legal, with separate rules and prices.

## Is street prostitution legal in the Netherlands?

Only inside a municipally designated tippelzone with set hours, and most cities have closed theirs. Anyone offering paid sex on the street outside such a zone is operating illegally — walk on.
