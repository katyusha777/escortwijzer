Hiring an escort in Eindhoven is legal: sex work between consenting adults is permitted throughout the Netherlands, and Eindhoven licenses escort businesses under article 3.2.1 of its municipal by-law, the APV. Unlike Rotterdam, Eindhoven does have a window district — but only one, and a small one, and most visitors end up booking online instead.

That window district is Baekelandplein, the single place in the city where window prostitution is allowed. Everything else runs by appointment: escorts to your hotel or apartment, and independents receiving at private addresses. This guide covers what the city requires, how Baekelandplein actually works for a visitor, and what a booking realistically costs.

## Is escort legal in Eindhoven?

Yes, under municipal licensing. The Netherlands legalised the operation of sex businesses in 2000, and article 3.2.1 of Eindhoven's APV (*Algemene Plaatselijke Verordening*, the general municipal by-law) prohibits running a sex establishment or escort business without a permit from the city. An agency operating without that licence is breaking the law — the client booking through it generally is not, but you'd be funding the illegal end of a legal market.

Two local rules worth knowing:

- **Minimum age 21.** Licensed Eindhoven businesses may only work with sex workers aged 21 or over — stricter than the national minimum of 18, and a rule shared with the big cities. Employing anyone younger is grounds to revoke the permit.
- **Street prostitution is tightly restricted.** It is allowed only in designated places, at set times, and with a valid access pass (*toegangsbewijs*, art. 3.2.6). Anything outside that framework is illegal — so ignore street offers entirely.

The Eindhoven by-law says nothing about working from home, unlike Utrecht or Rotterdam. That means there is no published home-work rule we could verify, not that it is settled either way — so we don't state one. For the national legal picture — what is actually criminal, how age and coercion rules work — see our explainer on [whether escorts are legal in the Netherlands](/en/law/is-escort-legal-netherlands/).

## What is Baekelandplein?

Baekelandplein is Eindhoven's only window location — the city has concentrated all window prostitution on this single square since 2005. It has 25 buildings and, at full occupancy, room for a maximum of around 50 sex workers, with at most two per building.

The city caps the operators hard: a maximum of 13 operating permits, and when there are more applicants than permits they are handed out not first-come-first-served but by lottery (Baekelandplein policy rules, 2023). A draw literally decides who may run a window — an unusually fair, and rare, way to allocate sex-work permits in the Netherlands.

What a visitor should know: this is not De Wallen. It is a compact square on the edge of the centre, not a quarter to wander through, and in practice many windows sit empty — the window capacity is tiny next to what's available online. If you want choice or any way to check who you're meeting in advance, the windows won't give it to you.

## How is the rest of the scene organised?

Away from Baekelandplein, everything runs by appointment, in two formats — and both work in English, as the Dutch escort scene does every day.

**Outcall escort.** The escort comes to your hotel or apartment. Booked through a licensed agency or directly with an independent on an advertising platform. This is the standard visitor booking and the largest segment in the city.

**Incall / privéontvangst.** You go to the escort — a working apartment or their own place. *Privéontvangst* means "private reception" at the worker's own address, usually the cheapest option because no agency margin or travel time is priced in. The differences between escorts, private reception and windows are laid out in our [guide to the Dutch formats](/en/law/escort-brothel-windows-difference/).

Because the window supply is so small, the online ad market *is* effectively the whole market — and that cuts both ways. Every fake profile in the Netherlands can claim to be in Eindhoven, and there's no physical place to verify who actually works there. ## How much does an escort cost in Eindhoven?

Expect roughly €130 to €180 per hour for an independent escort — the lower-to-middle end of the national band. Eindhoven is a regional city without the tourist premium that pushes Amsterdam rates €50 to €100 per hour higher. Agencies charge more, with screening and mediation built in, and a hotel outcall adds a €30 to €80 travel surcharge, standard nationwide.

Honest caveat on that figure: we have no live Eindhoven dataset. This band is derived from the national framework in our [Dutch escort price guide](/en/guides/escort-prices-netherlands/) plus the absence of a big-city tourist premium, not from locally measured numbers. Treat it as a guide, not a quote.

One number holds everywhere, here included: below €100 per hour, an ad is almost never real. Dutch taxes, advertising and workspace costs make legal work at that rate impossible, so a bargain-priced "Eindhoven" profile is bait pricing or a deposit scam.

## Can an escort visit my hotel in Eindhoven?

Almost always. Eindhoven is a business city — Brainport, home to Philips and ASML in the region, and a High Tech Campus where hundreds of companies and thousands of international knowledge workers are based. It has thousands of hotel rooms built for conference and business guests, and those hotels are used to visitors on the room. An escort arriving in the evening blends into the traffic.

Two practical points. Budget hotels and hostels sometimes run a strict guest policy with mandatory reception check-in — glancing at the house rules first saves an awkward lobby conversation. And expect screening in both directions: a professional will ask your hotel name, room number and the name on the booking. That's not suspicion, it's standard practice, and a better sign than someone who asks nothing at all.

Payment is cash at the start of the appointment, in euros; some escorts take a card or Dutch payment app on the spot. What nobody legitimate does is demand prepayment by payment link, gift card or crypto before you've met — that is the single most common escort scam aimed at foreigners, and our guide to [spotting fake escort profiles](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/) shows how to filter it out.

## Baekelandplein or online: which is smarter?

For most visitors: online. That's an opinion, so here's the reasoning. Baekelandplein has a hard ceiling of about 50 working spots and is rarely full; you see who's there on the spot, but you can't arrange anything in advance, read anything, or screen anyone. The online market is larger, gives you profiles to assess beforehand, and works by appointment — exactly what a discreet booking needs. The windows exist for the anonymity of simply walking past; anyone who wants choice and certainty books ahead.

The honest trade-off, whichever route you take: Eindhoven keeps no public register of licensed escort agencies, so you can't independently verify a permit as a client. You depend on what the agency or platform shows you and on the escort's online footprint — a weakness of the system that matters more here because the one window district barely functions as an alternative.
