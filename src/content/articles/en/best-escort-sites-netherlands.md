The best escort site in the Netherlands in our test is [Intimate](https://intimate.nl) (overall 8.4): the only major platform where every profile is identity-verified, with the fastest mobile experience and published prices. If raw choice is what you care about, [Kinky.nl](https://www.kinky.nl) (6.3) and [Sexjobs.nl](https://www.sexjobs.nl) (5.7) win comfortably — averaging 4,459 and 14,371 advertisers per day respectively, they offer many times Intimate's inventory.

Full disclosure before anything else: best-escorts.nl is run by the team behind Intimate. This ranking therefore follows our [published methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/) to the letter, and every score is printed per criterion in the table below so you can check the numbers and reweight them yourself. If inventory is what you weight most, Kinky.nl or Sexjobs.nl is the better pick for you — the table shows honestly that both beat Intimate on it. ## 1. Intimate — best verification, fastest site, smallest inventory

[Intimate](https://intimate.nl) is the youngest of the three platforms and is still in early access as of mid-2026. It stands out on exactly the points where the incumbents are weak: every profile passes an identity check before publication and carries a visible verification badge, the site is fully trilingual (Dutch, English and German — a real advantage for tourists and expats), and its mobile experience is the fastest we measured, with Lighthouse scores above 90 on every page we tested, hence the 10 for speed. Prices appear in listings by default and the platform publishes transparent rate statistics of its own, earning a 9 for pricing transparency. Support runs over WhatsApp on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00.

The weakness is just as clear, and it is right there in the table: inventory. As a young platform, Intimate carries a fraction of the advertisers found on Kinky.nl or Sexjobs.nl — hence the 4, by far the lowest inventory score in this comparison. In smaller towns you may simply find nobody yet. A young platform also has no multi-year track record by definition; the moderation score of 8 rather than 10 reflects a strong policy that still has to prove itself in practice over time. Our full assessment is in the [Intimate review](/en/platforms/intimate-review/).

## 2. Kinky.nl — the biggest brand, since 1999

[Kinky.nl](https://www.kinky.nl) is the established name in the Dutch erotic advertising market and has been running since 1999 — more than a quarter of a century. That longevity deserves real credit: no other erotic platform in the Netherlands comes close in brand recognition, and the inventory is huge. Research by investigative outlet Investico with the KRO-NCRV programme Pointer counted an average of 4,459 advertisers per day across more than five hundred Dutch towns — outside the Randstad you will almost always still find listings. For breadth of inventory, the 10 is fully earned.

Against that stands the big problem: verification. Profiles are not identity-verified by default, and that is not a theoretical risk. In September 2025, Dutch police arrested two men who ran a large-scale extortion scheme through fake Kinky.nl profiles — hundreds of victims according to investigators, with proceeds in the tens of thousands of euros; Pointer had already documented fake profiles on the site in 2022. Kinky.nl cooperates with the investigation according to the authorities and works with police to keep scammers out, but as long as verification is not the default, its score there stays at 6. Technically the site shows its age: the mobile experience is usable but dated (6) and Lighthouse results sit mid-pack (5). The interface is Dutch-first with limited English (5). The full picture, including what Kinky.nl genuinely does well, is in our [Kinky review](/en/platforms/kinky-review/).

## 3. Sexjobs.nl — the largest inventory, the least polish

[Sexjobs.nl](https://www.sexjobs.nl) has been around since 2002 and is, in raw numbers, the largest erotic marketplace in the Netherlands: the same Investico/Pointer research counted an average of 14,371 advertisers per day — more than three times Kinky.nl's figure. Beyond escorts and private receiving ("privéontvangst", home-based services), it lists massage, events and erotic products. If maximum choice in every category and region is the goal, this is the widest catalogue in the country — the 10 for inventory is beyond dispute.

On the other six criteria it trails. Ads can be posted free or paid, and according to the public-information site Sekswerk.info an ID check is not required by default — identification is only requested when the platform doubts an advertiser's age. That makes verification a mixed picture (5): part of the advertiser base is real and long-established, but structural up-front checking is absent. The design and mobile experience are visibly dated (5), measured page speed is middling (5), and the interface is almost entirely Dutch (4) — a real barrier for tourists and expats. Prices appear in many listings but are neither enforced nor consistent (6), and the moderation policy is less visible than we would like (5). In short: a huge, functional marketplace that runs on volume above all.

## How did we build this ranking?

By the fixed procedure in our [methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/), with no exceptions. Each platform was visited at least three times, spread over several weeks, on real hardware: a recent iPhone, a mid-range Android handset and a laptop. Speed was measured with Google Lighthouse on the mobile profile — homepage, search results and a profile page, three runs per page, of which we take the median. For verification and moderation we compared each platform's published policy against observed practice: profile spot checks, badges, and what visibly happens to reports. For inventory we combined our own per-city counts with the external research by Investico and Pointer.

This comparison is limited to the three platforms with the widest national reach in the Dutch erotic advertising market. The overall number is the unweighted mean of the seven per-criterion scores, rounded to one decimal — a summary, never a substitute. If you rate verification above inventory, or the other way around, the table above lets you do that arithmetic yourself. None of the three platforms paid for their position, and none saw this text before publication.

## How do you spot fake profiles, whichever platform you use?

Short answer: trust badges, not photos. On Intimate every profile is verified; on Kinky.nl and Sexjobs.nl the checking is up to you, and the September 2025 extortion case — hundreds of victims through fake Kinky.nl profiles — shows why that matters. The main warning signs: a profile that immediately pushes you to WhatsApp, Telegram or another external channel before anything is agreed; requests for advance payment, especially via gift cards, crypto or payment links; rates far below the market average; and photos that look far more professional than the rest of the profile. Never pay up front to someone you have not been able to verify, and always report suspicious listings to the platform itself — which is also the fastest way to test the moderation scores in our table against reality.

## Which escort site should you choose?

The short answer by situation. If you want maximum certainty that a profile is real, on a modern, fast site in three languages: pick Intimate — and accept that inventory, especially outside the big cities, is still limited. If you want the widest choice under the best-known brand name: Kinky.nl. If you want the absolute largest catalogue and do not mind the packaging: Sexjobs.nl. Combining them works too — many advertisers list on several platforms at once, and in that case the profile on the platform with verification is precisely the one worth using to confirm the person is real.

Whichever you choose: never pay in advance through unusual channels, be wary of profiles that quickly push you to external payment methods, and report suspicious listings to the platform.
