## Researched specifics (with sources)
1. **A different licensed form.** An erotische massagesalon is one of the five seksbedrijf forms that need a municipal exploitatievergunning — listed alongside brothels, escort agencies, window businesses and sex cinemas. Source: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/vergunning-seksbedrijf/). Cross-check forms explainer (already lists erotische massagesalon as a licensed form).
2. **The legal line (sexual vs non-sexual).** Erotic massage "comes both licensed and unlicensed" depending on municipality — a salon offering sexual services needs a seksinrichting/seksbedrijf vergunning; a genuinely non-sexual therapeutic massage does not. Source: [Sekswerkerfgoed](https://sekswerkerfgoed.nl/vormen-van-prostitutie-een-overzicht/). Cross-check woordenboek.md: "Massagesalon (+)" — only legal with a seksbedrijfvergunning; salons offering it without one operate illegally.
3. **How it differs from escort/privéontvangst.** Escort = worker travels to you; privéontvangst = you go to the worker's address; massage salon = a licensed premises where a masseur/masseuse provides massage (with erotic element). Different form, different licence line. Cross-check forms explainer.
4. **Definitions (link glossary).** body-to-body = masseur uses their whole body; nuru = full-body massage originating in Yokohama, Japan (1990s), using a slippery, water-soluble gel (traditionally seaweed-derived; now typically sodium polyacrylate) for skin-to-skin contact. Source: [Wikipedia — Nuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuru_(massage)). "Happy ending" = a massage ending in manual stimulation — a colloquial, not a legal, term. tantra = a slower, ritualised style framed around breath and full-body sensation rather than a goal.
5. **Price bands (verified, honest framing).** Tantra sessions: ~€155/hr, ~€225/1.5hr, ~€295/2hr (Pure Nature Passion); couples/four-hands run €300–€400/hr. Simpler erotic/body-to-body salon massage typically sits lower, often ~€80–€150/hr. Source: web search of Dutch salon price lists (purenature.nl, tantra providers), mid-2026. Frame honestly: shorter/simpler sessions are typically below escort's €150–€250/hr base; tantra at the top of the range meets or exceeds it. Link escort-prijzen.
6. **Legitimacy signals.** A licensed salon vs an unlicensed front: fixed premises, published prices, a real business name; unlicensed fronts advertise "massage +" without a licence and shade into escort-style scams. Link nepprofielen + verificatie.

## Opinion (with reason)
For someone who wants a lower-commitment, non-penetrative experience, a licensed massage salon is a legitimate and often cheaper entry point than an escort — but only a licensed one, because the unlicensed "massage +" ad is where the scams and the legally grey operations live. Reason: the licence is the single checkable signal separating a regulated business from a front.

## Honest limitation
The sexual/non-sexual line is genuinely blurry and municipality-dependent, and there's no public register you can check per salon — so from an ad alone you often can't tell a licensed erotic salon from an unlicensed one, or from a scam using "massage" as bait.
