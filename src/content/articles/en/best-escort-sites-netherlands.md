## 1. Intimate — best verification, fastest site, smallest inventory

[Intimate](https://intimate.nl) is the youngest platform in this comparison and is still in early access as of mid-2026. It stands out on exactly the points where the incumbents are weak: every profile passes an identity check before publication and carries a visible verification badge, the site is fully trilingual (Dutch, English and German — a real advantage for tourists and expats), and its mobile experience is the fastest we measured, with Lighthouse scores above 90 on every page we tested, hence the 10 for speed. Prices appear in listings by default and the platform publishes transparent rate statistics of its own, earning a 9 for pricing transparency. Support runs over WhatsApp on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00.

The weakness is just as clear, and it is right there in the table: inventory. As a young platform, Intimate carries a fraction of the advertisers found on Kinky.nl or Sexjobs.nl — hence the 4; only Tryst scores lower on inventory. In smaller towns you may simply find nobody yet. A young platform also has no multi-year track record by definition; the moderation score of 8 rather than 10 reflects a strong policy that still has to prove itself in practice over time. Our full assessment is in the [Intimate review](/en/platforms/intimate-review/).

## 2. Tryst — the strictest verification after Intimate, but not built for the Netherlands

[Tryst](https://tryst.link) is the odd one out in this ranking: an international directory run by Assembly Four — a social enterprise of sex workers and technologists based in Melbourne — where every single ad, including the free tier, passes photo verification and a manual human review before it goes live. That earns a 9 for verification, modern technology (8 for both mobile and speed), and openly published policies and advertiser prices. No Dutch marketplace comes close on any of that.

The second place deserves an honest footnote, because the caveats are substantial. The site is English-only, its Dutch section held just 112 profiles at test time — nearly all of them in Amsterdam — and rates displayed to us in US dollars. That Tryst still finishes above Kinky.nl says exactly what this ranking rewards: verification and product quality, not the thickness of the catalogue. If you are searching outside Amsterdam or want a Dutch-language site, you can skip Tryst without missing anything; the full picture is in our [Tryst review](/en/platforms/tryst-review/).

## 3. Kinky.nl — the biggest brand, since 1999

[Kinky.nl](https://www.kinky.nl) is the established name in the Dutch erotic advertising market and has been running since 1999 — more than a quarter of a century. That longevity deserves real credit: no other erotic platform in the Netherlands comes close in brand recognition, and the inventory is huge. Research by investigative outlet Investico with the KRO-NCRV programme Pointer counted an average of 4,459 advertisers per day across more than five hundred Dutch towns — outside the Randstad you will almost always still find listings. For breadth of inventory, the 10 is fully earned.

Against that stands the big problem: verification. Profiles are not identity-verified by default, and that is not a theoretical risk. In September 2025, Dutch police arrested two men who ran a large-scale extortion scheme through fake Kinky.nl profiles — hundreds of victims according to investigators, with proceeds in the tens of thousands of euros; Pointer had already documented fake profiles on the site in 2022. Kinky.nl cooperates with the investigation according to the authorities and works with police to keep scammers out, but as long as verification is not the default, its score there stays at 6. Technically the site shows its age: the mobile experience is usable but dated (6) and Lighthouse results sit mid-pack (5). The interface is Dutch-first with limited English (5). The full picture, including what Kinky.nl genuinely does well, is in our [Kinky review](/en/platforms/kinky-review/).

## How did we build this ranking?

By the fixed procedure in our [methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/), with no exceptions. Each platform was visited at least three times, spread over several weeks, on real hardware: a recent iPhone, a mid-range Android handset and a laptop. Speed was measured with Google Lighthouse on the mobile profile — homepage, search results and a profile page, three runs per page, of which we take the median. For verification and moderation we compared each platform's published policy against observed practice: profile spot checks, badges, and what visibly happens to reports. For inventory we combined our own per-city counts with the external research by Investico and Pointer.

This comparison covers nine platforms: the three with the widest national reach, plus six smaller and international players we have since reviewed in full. The overall number is the unweighted mean of the seven per-criterion scores, rounded to one decimal — a summary, never a substitute. Where two platforms tie, as Sexjobs and Boys4u do at 5.7, we rank the general-market platform above the niche one: Sexjobs serves every visitor, Boys4u only those looking for a male escort. If you rate verification above inventory, or the other way around, the table above lets you do that arithmetic yourself. None of the nine platforms paid for their position, and none saw this text before publication.

## How do you spot fake profiles, whichever platform you use?

Short answer: trust badges, not photos. On Intimate and Tryst every profile is checked before publication; on the open marketplaces the checking is up to you, and the September 2025 extortion case — hundreds of victims through fake Kinky.nl profiles — shows why that matters. The main warning signs: a profile that immediately pushes you to WhatsApp, Telegram or another external channel before anything is agreed; requests for advance payment, especially via gift cards, crypto or payment links; rates far below the market average; and photos that look far more professional than the rest of the profile. Never pay up front to someone you have not been able to verify, and always report suspicious listings to the platform itself — which is also the fastest way to test the moderation scores in our table against reality.

## Which escort site should you choose?

The short answer by situation. Maximum certainty that a profile is real, on a modern site in three languages: Intimate — and accept that inventory outside the big cities is still limited. The widest choice under the best-known brand name: Kinky.nl. The absolute largest catalogue, packaging irrelevant: Sexjobs.nl. English-speaking, searching mainly in Amsterdam, with verification as a hard requirement: Tryst. Free browsing across the Netherlands and Flanders: Redlights. Looking for a male escort: Boys4u — nowhere else bundles that inventory. EuroGirlsEscort and EscortNews are pre-trip browsing material at best, and EscortHub you skip.

Combining platforms works too — many advertisers list on several at once, and in that case the profile on a platform with verification is precisely the one worth using to confirm the person is real. Whichever you choose: never pay in advance through unusual channels, be wary of profiles that quickly push you to external payment methods, and report suspicious listings to the platform.
