## Opinion
Redlights is the most legible of the open marketplaces: it documents in plain help pages what its label does and does not check, and it reviews every ad before publication. But the "geverifieerd" label verifies photos, not people — calling that verification oversells it, and the free-entry model carries the same fake-ad risk as Kinky and Sexjobs.

## Flaw in every recommendation
- Redlights itself: label ≠ ID check; "grootste van Nederland" claim is unverifiable (no published counts; Investico counted 14,371/day on Sexjobs, no comparable Redlights figure); prices buried in ad texts.
- Kinky (alternative): verification optional, documented fraud cases.
- Sexjobs (alternative): checks on suspicion only, dated product.
- Intimate (alternative): small inventory, young platform.

## Credit where strong
Moderation legibility (pre-publication review with published turnaround times — better documented than Kinky/Sexjobs), NL+EN interface, Belgium coverage, breadth of categories incl. gigolo/fetish/koppels.

## Verdict (frontmatter, localized)
Open marketplace for NL+BE with the best-documented moderation of the free platforms; the verified label checks photos, not identity. Choose it for Belgium coverage and legibility, not for certainty.
