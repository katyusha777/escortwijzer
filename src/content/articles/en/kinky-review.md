Kinky.nl is the biggest adult advertising platform in the Benelux: online since 1999, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors a month, with listings in virtually every Dutch and Belgian city. If you want maximum choice, you start here — no other platform comes close on volume. The trade-off: verification is optional, and both investigative journalism and police cases show that fake profiles are a recurring problem. Our scores reflect exactly that split — a 10 for inventory, middling 6s for the trust features.

**Disclosure up front:** best-escorts.nl is run by the team behind Intimate.nl — a direct competitor of Kinky.nl. That is precisely why this review sticks to our [published methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/) with per-criterion scores you can check for yourself, and why Kinky gets full credit where it earns it: inventory, brand recognition and a quarter-century track record. A hit piece would be useless to you as a reader — and unfair to the platform that effectively built the Dutch market.

## What is Kinky.nl?

Kinky.nl is a classifieds marketplace for the erotic sector. Sex workers, escorts, massage studios, clubs and related businesses place ads; visitors contact them directly. The platform does not broker appointments — think of it as the adult-industry equivalent of a general classifieds site, operating within the Dutch legal framework where sex work is legal and regulated.

The site went live in 1999, which makes it older than most of the internet companies you use daily. Over 25-plus years it grew into what comparison sites and industry media consistently describe as one of the largest digital marketplaces for the erotic sector in both the Netherlands and Belgium, with hundreds of thousands of unique monthly visitors. That longevity matters: in a sector where platforms appear and vanish constantly, 25 years of continuity builds trust with advertisers and visitors alike. Practically every sex worker in the Netherlands knows the platform; many clients know no other.

## How good is verification?

Middling — verification exists but is optional, hence a 6. Kinky.nl offers verification symbols that advertisers can earn to prove they are real, and the platform advises visitors to look for those symbols and read reviews. That is exactly the problem: the burden of screening sits with the visitor, not the platform.

The consequences are on record. In February 2022, investigative programme Pointer (KRO-NCRV) counted over 700 misleading sex ads across Kinky and Sexjobs combined, tied to 394 phone numbers flagged for scams or extortion on a Dutch caller-reporting site. In September 2025, Dutch police arrested two men who had used fake profiles on Kinky.nl to extort hundreds of victims, with damages running into tens of thousands of euros. Kinky cooperated with the investigation, but the pattern is clear: on an open platform of this size, fake profiles get through. Verified listings are dependable enough — the unverified portion demonstrably is not always.

## How is the mobile experience?

Functional but dated: a 6. Kinky.nl works on mobile through the browser and the site adapts to smaller screens. There is no native app — Apple and Google ban adult content from their app stores, so that limitation applies to the entire sector. Within that constraint, Kinky does the job: searching, filtering and viewing ads all work on a phone. It just never feels like a modern mobile product; the interface visibly carries the legacy of two decades of incremental building on the same foundation. Anyone used to well-designed modern web apps will notice the difference immediately.

## How fast is the site?

The weakest point in our testing: a 5. At the time of review, our test setup was served a Cloudflare bot-verification screen before the site would load — understandable as anti-scraping protection, but it noticeably delays first load. Beyond that, Kinky is no speed champion: pages are heavy with ads, images and promotional blocks loading at once. On a fast connection it is acceptable; on mobile data it feels slow. For a platform with this much traffic there is clear room for optimisation, and competitors built on modern, lighter codebases demonstrably load faster.

## How transparent is pricing?

Reasonable, with no guarantees: a 6. Advertisers set their own rates and many listings state them — comparison sites cite €50 to €100 as a common range for a booking arranged through the platform. But there is no mandatory rate field, no platform-wide price statistics, and no check that the advertised rate matches what is asked in person. Surprise add-ons and "price changes at the door" are a recurring complaint in user reviews. Kinky gives you a decent feel for the market, but you need to confirm per listing exactly what a stated price includes.

## How good is moderation?

Present, but reactive: a 6. Kinky has a moderation team, removes rule-breaking ads and cooperated with police and journalists in the documented fraud cases. That is more than nothing — but the cases above show moderation mostly happens after the fact, once victims have surfaced or media start asking questions. At Kinky's volume, proactively vetting every listing is barely feasible without mandatory upfront verification; keeping the barrier to entry low is a structural choice the platform has made. That choice explains both the enormous inventory and the recurring incidents. An honest assessment has to count both.

## What languages does Kinky.nl support?

Kinky is a Dutch-language platform, and it shows: a 5. The interface and the vast majority of listings are in Dutch, aimed at the Netherlands and Flanders. For tourists and expats — a substantial share of demand in cities like Amsterdam — that is a genuine barrier: if you do not read Dutch, you are running listings through machine translation and missing nuance in service descriptions and terms. Some advertisers write in English, but that is their individual choice, not a platform feature.

## How big is the inventory?

The best in the market, no contest: a 10. Kinky.nl carries the largest inventory of adult listings in the Benelux — escorts, private visits, massage, BDSM, cam and more, across virtually every city and region in the Netherlands and Belgium. At the time of review, no Dutch platform came anywhere near it on volume. For advertisers that means the biggest audience; for visitors, the widest choice. This is why Kinky is still the default after 25 years: the network effect of the largest inventory meeting the largest audience reinforces itself, and no newcomer has an answer to it yet.

## Who is Kinky.nl best for?

Kinky is the right choice if you want maximum choice, if you are searching in smaller cities where niche platforms have no coverage, or if you want to survey the whole market before contacting anyone. Dutch speakers who know [how to spot fake listings](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/) will get the most out of it. It is a poorer fit for non-Dutch speakers, and for anyone who wants guaranteed-verified profiles without doing their own screening.
