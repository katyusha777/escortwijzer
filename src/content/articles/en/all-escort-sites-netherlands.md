Nineteen active escort sites serve the Dutch market as of August 2026: eight Dutch platforms, five international directories with a genuine Netherlands section, and six niche sites. The biggest by far is [Sexjobs.nl](https://www.sexjobs.nl) at an average of 14,371 advertisers per day ([Investico/Pointer](https://www.platform-investico.nl/onderzoeken/op-dit-huisnummer-woont-een-prostituee)); the only Dutch platform that identity-verifies every profile is [Intimate](https://intimate.nl).

Disclosure first: best-escorts.nl is made by the team behind Intimate. That is exactly why this page is a plain inventory — every site is listed, every site gets its own outbound link, and where we have a full scored review, we link it. How we score is documented in our [methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/); if you want a ranking rather than a list, read [the best escort sites in the Netherlands](/en/platforms/best-escort-sites-netherlands/).

One thing to know before the list: sex work is legal and regulated in the Netherlands, and these sites operate openly. But most of the market speaks Dutch. If you are visiting, the practical shortlist is short, and we flag the English-friendly options as we go.

Method: we visited every site directly in August 2026 and checked for current ads, cross-referenced against the platform list kept by [Sekswerk.info](https://www.sekswerk.info/en/work/getting-started-as-a-sex-worker/finding-clients-online), the government-funded information site for sex workers. Dead, parked and dubious sites are listed separately at the bottom, because outdated "top 10" pages still send people there.

What counts as an "escort site" here? Ad platforms and directories — places where multiple independent workers or agencies advertise. The hundreds of websites run by individual licensed escort agencies fall outside this list; that is a separate category, recognisable by the agency's own licence number displayed on the site. Pure dating and webcam platforms are also excluded, though the border gets blurry in places; where it matters, we say so.

## Which Dutch platforms are there?

**[Kinky.nl](https://www.kinky.nl)** has been the household name since 1999, averaging 4,459 advertisers per day across more than five hundred towns. Profiles are not verified by default, and that mattered: a September 2025 extortion case ran through fake profiles on the site, with hundreds of victims. The interface is Dutch-first with limited English. Full assessment in our [Kinky review](/en/platforms/kinky-review/).

**[Sexjobs.nl](https://www.sexjobs.nl)** has run since 2002 and is the country's largest marketplace in raw numbers. Beyond escorts and private receiving (privéontvangst) it lists massage and adult products; per Sekswerk.info, ID checks happen only when age is in doubt. Almost entirely Dutch-language — workable with browser translation, not built for you. See the [Sexjobs review](/en/platforms/sexjobs-review/).

**[Intimate](https://intimate.nl)** is the youngest platform, still in early access in mid-2026. It is the only Dutch platform that verifies every profile before publication, runs natively in Dutch, English and German, and shows prices by default — but its inventory is a fraction of the two incumbents', and in smaller cities you may find nobody at all yet. Scores and screenshots in the [Intimate review](/en/platforms/intimate-review/).

**[Redlights.nl](https://www.redlights.nl)** is a mid-sized Dutch ad site with free and paid placements, named by Sekswerk.info among the usual advertising venues. Same model as the big two, smaller pond, Dutch-only. Our scores are in the [Redlights review](/en/platforms/redlights-review/).

**[Boys4u.nl](https://www.boys4u.nl)** is the Dutch platform for male sex workers. Trans advertisers rarely use it, according to Sekswerk.info; they are better served by the niche sites below. More in the [Boys4u review](/en/platforms/boys4u-review/).

**[Topescort.nl](https://www.topescort.nl)** is the Dutch arm of an international directory network, with free and paid ads. It comes with a fresh legal footnote: on 21 January 2026 the Council of State (Raad van State) annulled a penalty against operator Siteways ([ECLI:NL:RVS:2026:351](https://www.raadvanstate.nl/@156002/202304761-1-a3/)), finding insufficient evidence that the site was brokering escort work — which requires a licence — rather than merely hosting ads. That line between advertising and brokering is where every site in this article legally lives.

**[Eromarkt.nl](https://www.eromarkt.nl)** and its sister **[Escortmarkt.nl](https://www.escortmarkt.nl)** are free, bulletin-board style classifieds from the same small network. Eromarkt is broader (massage, BDSM categories, dating ads); Escortmarkt sticks to escorts and private receiving. Both had fresh ads when we checked, and neither has any verification or visible moderation. Dutch-only.

## Which international directories have a real Dutch section?

This is where English speakers actually land, and the quality varies more than the near-identical layouts suggest. Manage your expectations on inventory, though: these directories largely re-list the same advertisers you would find on the Dutch platforms, repackaged in English at international ad rates.

**[EuroGirlsEscort](https://www.eurogirlsescort.com)** carries the largest Netherlands section of the international directories, including a separate trans category, and uses photo-verification badges. See the [EuroGirlsEscort review](/en/platforms/eurogirlsescort-review/).

**[EscortNews](https://netherlands.escortnews.com)** runs a dedicated Netherlands subdomain inside a worldwide network; verification is partial and paid placement drives visibility. See the [EscortNews review](/en/platforms/escortnews-review/).

**[Tryst](https://tryst.link)** is the most interesting of this group: it identity-verifies every advertiser and was built by people from the sex-work community. Its Dutch section is small but real. Our assessment: the [Tryst review](/en/platforms/tryst-review/).

**[EscortHub](https://escorthub.org)** is a younger international directory with a modest, maintained Dutch section. See the [EscortHub review](/en/platforms/escorthub-review/).

**[EscortDirectory](https://www.escortdirectory.com)** showed roughly 75 Amsterdam ads when we counted in August 2026 and runs in seven languages — none of them Dutch, which tells you precisely who it is built for.

## What about gay, trans, massage and BDSM sites?

The gay market has its own infrastructure, largely separate from the general platforms. **[Hunqz](https://www.hunqz.com)** is the international leader, run from Amsterdam by the company behind Romeo, with an optional verification badge. **[RentMen](https://rentmen.eu)** and **[RentMasseur](https://www.rentmasseur.com)** both work on subscriptions with mandatory ID upload; the latter is massage-specific. The Dutch **[GayAds.nl](https://www.gayads.nl)** is a free marketplace for gay and trans advertisers, organised by province, Dutch-language.

Two odd ones out. **[Grenddo](https://www.grenddo.com)** is not an ad site but a Dutch work platform for sex workers — bookings, content sales and payments in one place, explicitly without storing identity documents. **[Bullchat](https://www.bullchat.nl)** is a Dutch gay chat site that Sekswerk.info notes is used informally for paid meets: anonymous, unverified, a channel rather than a platform.

And BDSM? A dedicated Dutch BDSM ad site does not currently exist. Dominatrixes and SM studios advertise in the BDSM categories of Kinky, Sexjobs and Eromarkt; anything promoted as a standalone "Dutch BDSM site" is almost certainly an affiliate doorway.

## Which sites are dead or dubious?

The part outdated lists leave out, and honestly the most useful section on this page.

**Speurders.nl** once ran around 31,000 erotic ads, making it the country's number three behind Sexjobs and Kinky. It closed its erotic section ([Investico](https://www.platform-investico.nl/onderzoeken/sekswerk-gaat-door-ondanks-het-virus)); in 2026 the old erotiek URL simply redirects to the homepage.

**Hookers.nl**, the Dutch review forum with 250,000 users, shut down in 2021. Owner Midhold pulled the plug over privacy after the Dutch Data Protection Authority found that reviewing sex workers without their consent violates the GDPR ([Pointer](https://pointer.kro-ncrv.nl/sekswerkers-en-klanten-maken-zich-zorgen-om-verdwijnen-prostitutieforum-hookersnl)). The domain still answers; the forum is gone.

**Sexadvertenties.nl** earns a specific warning: the site is live and looks like an ad platform, but its own entry terms state that it facilitates "erotic chat conversations between fictional profiles and users" and that meeting these profiles in person is not possible. You would be paying to chat with invented people.

**Pimponline**, still recommended in old alternatives round-ups, is dead: the .nl domain no longer resolves and the .com serves an empty page.

Three international names deliberately did not make our list. Skokka has no Dutch edition, Escort-Ads.com is almost entirely US-focused with no meaningful Dutch inventory, and we could not confirm current Dutch activity on TopEscortBabes or TS-Dating — and we do not list what we cannot verify.

One grey area to round it off: doorway sites. Dozens of thin "escort Amsterdam" pages carry no ads of their own and simply forward every click to one of the big directories for a commission. The tell is the missing "place your ad" button — a site that accepts no advertisers is not a platform.

## How do you choose from nineteen sites?

Our position, and the reason this page is a list rather than a sales pitch: more sites does not mean more choice. Outside the top platforms you mostly meet the same advertisers cross-posted everywhere, while the share of fakes rises — Pointer found scam signals in [over 700 of roughly 9,000 ads](https://pointer.kro-ncrv.nl/risico-op-oplichting-bij-ruim-700-online-seksadvertenties) on the two biggest sites alone. Two or three platforms cover the entire market; pick them by verification policy, not by the length of anyone's list.

You can turn that cross-posting to your advantage. If the same advertiser appears on an unverified site and also holds a verified profile on a platform that checks identity, that second profile is your authenticity check — free, and more reliable than any reverse image search. Never pay a deposit to a profile you cannot find verified anywhere.
