Hiring an escort in The Hague is legal: the Netherlands permits sex work between consenting adults, and the city licenses sex businesses — escort agencies included — with one-year permits at a €1,704 fee. The Hague is also one of the last Dutch cities outside Amsterdam with genuine window prostitution: the Doubletstraat and Geleenstraat were open as of mid-2026, having survived years of closure debate.

That makes The Hague the one Dutch city where a visitor faces a real choice of formats: the windows you can walk past tonight, or the escort you book to your hotel. They are different products at different prices, and this guide covers both — plus the licensing rules, the real state of the closure debate, and what an appointment actually costs in the city of embassies.

## Is escort legal in The Hague?

Yes, under the tightest licensing regime of the big four Dutch cities. Sex businesses — the municipality explicitly names sex clubs, escort agencies and erotic massage salons — need an operating permit (*exploitatievergunning*) that is granted for one year at a time, costs €1,704 in fees, and comes with an integrity screening of the operator repeated every four years. Sex workers at licensed Hague businesses must be at least 21 (the national minimum is 18) and registered with the Dutch chamber of commerce as self-employed professionals.

One more Hague quirk: since 1 October 1999 the city has run a fixed-maximum system. The number of sex establishments per location is capped, and a new business can only open by taking over an existing permit — which is why the map of licensed venues has barely changed in 25 years.

None of this creates obligations for you as a client. Booking an adult, consenting escort is lawful; the criminal lines are minors, coercion and unlicensed operators. ## Is the Doubletstraat still open — and for how long?

Open, yes — and after years of political wrestling, that is a considered decision rather than neglect. The short history, because most of what's online about it is outdated:

The Hague has two licensed window areas: Doubletstraat in the old centre, the larger with around 190 windows, and Geleenstraat plus the small adjacent Hunsestraat, together around 140. A third street, the Poeldijksestraat, closed in 2005 and was demolished. Doubletstraat carries the national reputation of being the cheapest window street in the Netherlands, with short visits cited from about €30.

Since 2021, successive city plans tried to move all of it into a purpose-built erotic centre — first near Central Station, later on the edge of the Binckhorst district. None proved feasible. The city then piloted three alternatives to the window: renting rooms by the hour, a shared work location, and digital client contact. All three failed — the hourly-rental pilot attracted not a single sex worker in six months, and workers rejected digital contact because the window lets them see exactly who is at the door before opening it. In October 2025 the council voted 23–20 to keep the windows open, and the May 2026 evaluation concluded there is no full alternative and that closure would cost tens of millions in buy-outs and compensation. The dossier now sits with the coalition formed after the March 2026 municipal elections, which had taken no decision by mid-2026.

Our view: keeping the windows open was the right call, for the unsentimental reason that the city's own pilots proved nothing exists to replace 330 licensed workplaces. Closing them without an alternative pushes the work into unlicensed apartments — the exact outcome licensing was invented to prevent.

## Windows or escort: which should a visitor choose?

They solve different problems, so choose by what you actually want.

**The windows** are direct: you see who's working, you negotiate at the door, you pay window prices — Doubletstraat's from-€30 short visits are the cheapest paid sex in the country. The trade-offs are equally direct: zero discretion (these are residential streets, not a themed district), short transactional visits, and an environment that is scruffier than Amsterdam's canal-side postcard. Tourists expecting De Wallen will find Doubletstraat sobering.

**Escorts** are the discreet track: booked in advance in English, visiting your hotel or receiving you at a private address, priced per hour. For business travellers, conference visitors and anyone staying in Scheveningen or the centre, this is the segment that fits. The legal and practical differences between the formats are laid out in our [guide to escorts, windows and brothels in the Netherlands](/en/law/escort-brothel-windows-difference/).

What the windows' prices don't do is predict escort rates. Different product, different market — the €30 figure has no bearing on what an hour with an independent escort costs.

## How much does an escort cost in The Hague?

Expect €150 to €230 per hour for an independent escort, with agencies starting around €250. That puts The Hague in the stable middle of the Dutch market: pricier than Rotterdam, clearly below Amsterdam's tourist-inflated €200–€350. The city's steady base of expat, diplomatic and conference demand keeps the mid-segment well supplied without an Amsterdam-style premium.

Standard Dutch mechanics apply: a €30–€80 outcall surcharge for hotel visits, cheaper per-hour rates for longer bookings, payment in cash at the start of the appointment (some accept a card or Dutch payment app on the spot), and no tipping expectation. The scam filter is the same as everywhere in the country: an ad far below €100 per hour is bait or fake, and anyone demanding advance payment by payment link, gift card or crypto before you've met is a fraudster, not an escort. ## Can an escort visit my hotel in The Hague?

Usually, yes. Hotel outcalls are the standard visitor booking, and hotels in the government quarter, around Central Station and along the Scheveningen beachfront all host year-round business and conference guests — a visitor arriving for a registered guest raises no eyebrows. Budget hotels and hostels are the exception; some require visitors to sign in at reception, so skim the house rules before booking an outcall.

Expect mutual screening: hotel name, room number, and the name your booking is under.
