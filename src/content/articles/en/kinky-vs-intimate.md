If you want maximum choice today, use Kinky.nl: it has run since 1999 and, according to research by investigative outlets Investico and Pointer, averages 4,459 advertisers per day across more than five hundred Dutch towns. If verified identity weighs heavier for you — and for visitors who don't read Dutch it usually should — Intimate wins: it is the only one of the two that identity-checks every profile before publication, and it scores 8.4 against Kinky's 6.3 in our test.

That's the whole answer in two sentences. The rest of this page shows where the numbers come from, criterion by criterion, so you can reweight them if your priorities differ.

Disclosure first, because this comparison demands it: escortwijzer.nl is run by the team behind Intimate. We are comparing our own platform against its biggest competitor here. That is exactly why we follow our [published methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/) to the letter, print all seven sub-scores in the table below, and name Intimate's weakness as bluntly as Kinky's. If inventory is your top criterion — a perfectly reasonable position — the conclusion of this page is simply: use Kinky.

One pattern stands out: these are not two similar platforms separated by details, but mirror images. Kinky wins one criterion by the maximum possible margin; Intimate wins the other six.

What they agree on matters just as much: both are advertising platforms, not agencies. You arrange everything directly with the advertiser, both operate openly within the Dutch legal framework — sex work is legal and regulated here — and neither plans, pays for or guarantees anything on your behalf. The difference sits entirely in what happens before a listing goes live.

## Verification: the biggest gap (6 vs 10)

On Kinky, verification exists but is optional — the checking is left to you. The consequences are documented: Pointer counted over 700 misleading ads across Kinky and Sexjobs in February 2022, and in September 2025 Dutch police arrested two men who had extorted hundreds of victims through fake Kinky.nl profiles.

On Intimate, verification is the front door: no listing goes live without an identity check. Advertisers submit four photos — an ID document (masked with the Dutch government's KopieID tool, including the citizen service number), a selfie holding that ID, proof of residence no more than a week old, and an unedited full-body photo — all reviewed manually by the moderation team. Minimum age is 21, above the legal minimum.

If you choose on this criterion alone, you're done reading. There is no scenario where Kinky wins it.

## Inventory: the biggest gap, the other way (10 vs 4)

Equally quick, in reverse. Kinky has the largest inventory in the Benelux: those 4,459 daily advertisers spread across five hundred towns, so even outside the Randstad you will almost always find someone. Intimate is in early access and lists only a few profiles in most cities.

This is Intimate's honestly named weakness, and it is not a small one: search in a mid-sized town and there is a real chance Intimate has nothing for you today while Kinky does. Mandatory verification slows growth deliberately — every new profile is real, but they arrive slower.

## Technology: speed and mobile (5 and 6 vs 10 and 9)

Here the age difference shows. Kinky carries 25 years of building on the same foundations: usable on a phone but dated, pages heavy with ad blocks, and at the time of our review our test setup hit a Cloudflare bot-verification screen before the site even loaded. Intimate was built as a modern, mobile-first product and was the fastest platform we tested, with Lighthouse scores above 90 on every page.

The honest caveat on Intimate's numbers: a young platform with few listings has little weight to carry. Whether the speed holds at Kinky's ad volume remains to be proven.

## Pricing, moderation and languages: the same pattern three times

On pricing transparency (6 vs 9), many Kinky listings state a rate but nothing is required or checked; different prices at the door are a recurring complaint. Intimate uses structured rate fields and filters, though not every advertiser fills in every field there either.

On moderation (6 vs 8), Kinky is mostly reactive: it cooperates with police and journalists, but intervention visibly happens after victims exist. Intimate checks every profile up front and runs a reachable WhatsApp helpdesk (weekdays 9:00–17:00) — but at its current volume it has barely been stress-tested, and moderation only proves itself under pressure. Hence an 8, not a 10.

On languages (5 vs 9), the difference matters most if you're reading this in English. Kinky is almost entirely Dutch: interface, listings, terms. Intimate has a translated interface plus a filter on the languages an advertiser actually speaks — Dutch, English, German, Spanish and Russian — so you know before making contact whether you can hold a conversation. For tourists and expats this is the difference between usable and not.

## Why are the two platforms so different?

Our opinion, with the reason attached: these are not accidental differences but two structural choices, each of which forces its own scorecard. Kinky's open model — advertising without mandatory identity checks — is precisely what makes the inventory enormous and what lets the fake profiles in; you cannot have one without the other. Intimate's mandatory verification works exactly the other way: maximum certainty, slow growth.

Which means "which platform is better?" is really the wrong question. The right question is which risk you would rather carry: the chance that a profile is fake, or the chance that your city has nothing listed.

## Which platform fits which situation?

**Choose Kinky.nl if:** you want maximum choice today; you're searching outside the big cities; you read Dutch and know how to [spot fake listings](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/). The price you pay: screening is your job, because the platform doesn't do it for you — and without Dutch, even that is hard.

**Choose Intimate if:** certainty about who is behind a profile outweighs choice; you don't speak Dutch; you want to filter by language or services before making contact. The price you pay: in many cities the inventory is still small or absent, and a young platform has no quarter-century track record.

**Advertising yourself?** The trade-off is the same, mirrored: Kinky offers the largest audience but also the largest crowd to stand out in — fake competition included. Intimate offers a smaller audience, but advertisers who pass verification compete only against real profiles.

**Use both if:** you want the best of each. Many advertisers list on multiple platforms; find someone on Kinky, then check whether the same person holds a verified profile on Intimate. It takes two minutes and beats every other authenticity check.
