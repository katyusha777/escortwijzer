## Researched specifics (fetch-verified 2026-08-01)
1. **Baseline legality**: prostitution legal between consenting adults — https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/prostitutie and https://www.government.nl/topics/prostitution ("Prostitution is legal in the Netherlands as long as it involves sex between consenting adults").
2. **Minors, post-2024 articles** (Wet seksuele misdrijven, in force 1 July 2024; replaced old art. 248b Sr, max 4 yrs):
   - Art. 245 Sr lid 1: seksuele handelingen with 16/17-year-old incl. the child "dat zich beschikbaar stelt tot het verrichten van seksuele handelingen … tegen betaling" → max 6 yrs / cat-4 fine; lid 2 (violence/threat) 8 yrs / cat 5.
   - Art. 246 Sr lid 1: same circumstances with seksueel binnendringen → max 9 yrs / cat 5; lid 2 → 12 yrs.
   - Text verified via https://wettekst.com/wetten/wetboek-van-strafrecht/artikel/245/ and /246/; official source cited in article: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0001854/2026-07-01
   - Under 16: art. 247–250 Sr (heavier), payment is statutory aggravation via art. 254 Sr (consistent with nl/is-escort-legaal.md).
   - Knowledge of age NOT required — client liable regardless (established in sibling article).
3. **Client of a trafficking victim — art. 273g Sr** (in force 1 Jan 2022): sexual acts with someone you "weet of ernstige reden [hebt] om te vermoeden" is a trafficking victim → max 4 yrs / cat-4 fine; 6 yrs if victim under 18. Verified via https://wettekst.com/wetten/wetboek-van-strafrecht/artikel/273g/
4. **OM richtlijn mensenhandel 2024R008** confirms art. 273g and art. 245/246 lid 1 onder d apply to clients of trafficking victims; sets NO separate sentencing demand for clients; sexual-exploitation demand for traffickers starts at 24 months per victim. — https://www.om.nl/onderwerpen/beleidsregels/richtlijnen-voor-strafvordering-resultaten/richtlijn-voor-strafvordering-mensenhandel-2024r008
5. **Trafficker penalties, art. 273f Sr**: 12 yrs base, 15 (minor victim/violence), 18 (serious injury), 30/life (death) — established with sources in nl/is-escort-legaal.md.
6. **Unlicensed booking is NOT a client crime today**: the WRS (bill 35.715) would have criminalised clients of unlicensed sex workers (plus national licence duty, min. age 21); cabinet announced spring 2026 it will not proceed (Kamerbrief 3 Apr 2026, established in sibling article). — https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/35715_wet_regulering_sekswerk
7. **21-years plan, 2026 state**: on 13 May 2026 cabinet announced raising the minimum age 18→21, investigating a pooierverbod, and submitting the Wgts (municipal oversight of sex businesses) to parliament shortly. — https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/05/13/kabinet-wil-minimumleeftijd-prostitutie-verhogen-naar-21-jaar-en-onderzoekt-pooierverbod — Government.nl states the client side explicitly: it "wants to make it a criminal offence to engage the services of a prostitute younger than 21". Still a plan, not law; national minimum remains 18.
8. **Municipal 21+ practice**: Amsterdam (since 2013), Rotterdam, The Hague already require 21 at licensed businesses (sibling article, sourced).

## Opinion (with reason)
We support raising the age to 21: the big-city licensed sector already works at 21, so national law would follow proven practice, and for clients the rule becomes simple (under 21 = don't book). Stated counterweight: 18–20-year-olds won't vanish, they shift to the unlicensed circuit with zero oversight, and no client can reliably tell 19 from 22 — so the gain depends on enforcement plus accessible licensed work, not the number alone.

## Flaws / gray zones to state honestly
- "Ernstige reden om te vermoeden" (273g) is undefined; no statutory signal list — liability turns on an open norm.
- Age is de facto strict liability for the client: criminal even if you couldn't have known.
- OM has no client-specific sentencing guideline → outcome of a prosecution is hard to predict.
- Paradox: booking unlicensed is legal, yet that is where the two real crimes (minors, coercion) concentrate.
