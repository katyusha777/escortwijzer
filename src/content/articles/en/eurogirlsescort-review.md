EuroGirlsEscort showed 4,297 listings for the Netherlands when we tested it, and exactly 691 of them — one in six — carried a Verified badge. The rest run unchecked, the sort order is for sale from about €8 per month, and that combination lands the directory on an overall 5.4 in our scoring.

**Disclosure up front:** best-escorts.nl is run by the team behind Intimate.nl, a Dutch platform competing for the same visitors this directory serves. That is why this review follows our [published methodology](/en/platforms/methodology/) with a checkable score per criterion — including the criteria where EuroGirlsEscort beats the Dutch platforms, because there are some.

## What is EuroGirlsEscort and who runs it?

EuroGirlsEscort is an international escort directory with country sections from Albania to Zambia; the Netherlands is one of over a hundred. The operator, per the site's own GDPR page, is Web No Limit s.r.o., a Czech company at Korunní 588/4 in Prague. The footer states the business model without embarrassment: "We ONLY sell advertisement space" — the site sells listings and disclaims all responsibility for what is in them.

Advertising is free. Visibility is not: TOP status costs 40 GOLD (about €8 per month), VIP costs 250 GOLD (about €50) and buys homepage placement, with VIP profiles "ranked as first" — the FAQ's own words — whenever a visitor uses filters.

That price list is our opinion of the site, reason included. The FAQ publishes the exact sorting ladder: PREMIUM TOP VERIFIED, then PREMIUM TOP UNVERIFIED, only then ordinary TOP VERIFIED, all the way down to "Ads OLDER THAN 10 MONTHS" at the bottom. Read it twice: a paying unverified ad outranks a free verified one, and ten-month-old ads are kept rather than deleted. The order you scroll through is a rate card, not a quality ranking.

## How good is the verification?

Voluntary and sparse: a 4. Verification means sending in a video, which staff review within a stated 48 hours. That is a more substantial check than a mere tick-box — but it is optional, and the Dutch section's own counters tell the story: 691 Verified ads out of roughly 4,300. There is even a separate "Only verified phone" filter (1,643), which mostly confirms that for the majority not even the phone number has been checked.

Just as relevant for a visitor to the Netherlands: the video confirms that the person in the photos exists. It is not a document-based identity or age check of the kind Dutch licensing rules are built around. The site describes unverified ads with the phrase "we are not able to confirm the authenticity of the Ad" — and then publishes them anyway, slightly lower on the page.

## How is the mobile experience?

Usable, with one nasty habit: a 5. The card layout works on a phone and the filters are reachable. But during our test session the browser was redirected mid-browse to an unrelated third-party ad site — the kind of ad script that is even more hostile on a phone than on a desktop. One redirect like that says more about the site's advertising trade than ten tidy product pages.

## How fast is the site?

Mid-pack: a 5. Listing pages load a large batch of images at once and carry third-party ad scripts. Acceptable on wifi, sluggish on mobile data — and the redirects mentioned above cost more time than any loading bar.

## How transparent is the pricing?

The strongest criterion: a 7. Profiles carry a real rates table per duration, split into incall and outcall — one Amsterdam profile we opened listed 200 EUR for an outcall hour — plus an itemised list of priced extras. The search filters even show price bands with counts: 3,480 Dutch listings sat in the €151–300-per-hour band during our test. That is more up-front price information than Kinky or Sexjobs shows by default.

The usual caveat stands: nobody checks that the rate in the table is the rate at the door.

## How good is the moderation?

Weak: a 4. There is something — the FAQ bans fake photos and duplicate profiles, there is a Black list page and a phishing warning. But the sorting rules keep ads older than ten months at the bottom of the list instead of removing them, and the "Last seen online" field on profiles shows how long some advertisers have been gone. A directory that sells positions while declaring it only sells ad space has little incentive to sweep hard. And nowhere on the site is there any trace of awareness that Dutch sex work is a licensed, municipally regulated industry.

## Which languages does EuroGirlsEscort support?

Seven, and this is where the directory earns its keep for tourists: a 7. The interface switches between English, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Italian and Czech — a genuinely complete English UI, which is exactly what the big Dutch platforms fail to offer. If you are landing at Schiphol without a word of Dutch, this is why you found this site first.

The omission is still telling: there is no Dutch version at all. A directory showing 4,300 Dutch listings without serving a single Dutch-speaking visitor has decided who its product is for — and it is not the local market.

## How big is the Dutch inventory?

Big on paper: a 6. The counter says 4,297 — the same order of magnitude as Kinky.nl's measured average of 4,459 advertisers per day. But the directory has exactly ten Dutch city pages (Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague, Utrecht, Eindhoven, Groningen, Haarlem, Maastricht, Tilburg, Breda), where Kinky covers over five hundred towns. A large slice is travelling inventory too: 1,603 ads are set to "Countrywide" availability and another thousand to Europe or worldwide. Add the ten-month retention policy and the genuinely active, genuinely local number sits well below the headline figure.

## Who is EuroGirlsEscort best for?

The English-speaking visitor browsing before a trip: comparing rates, shortlisting, deciding later. For that job it works — full English interface, price tables, filters that do what they say.

If you intend to actually book from it, do the homework the directory skips. Reverse-image-search the photos, check whether the phone number appears elsewhere, never pay anything in advance — the full checklist is in our guide to [spotting fake escort profiles](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/). And cross-check the name on a Dutch platform: if the same person holds a verified profile there, you know more than any badge here can tell you.
