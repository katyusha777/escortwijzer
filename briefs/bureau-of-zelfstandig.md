## The question
The client's choice between an escort agency (escortbureau) and an independent (zelfstandige) escort — reliability/recourse vs price/direct contact.

## Researched specifics (with sources)
1. **What a licensed agency does + who needs the licence.** The licence obligation sits with the mediator: if someone manages your website or answers your phone, that intermediary needs an escortbureau licence. A fully independent escort — who advertises, arranges contact, makes appointments and arranges transport herself — needs no licence. Source: [ACS — Vergunning nodig?](https://amsterdamcenterforsexworkers.nl/voor-sekswerkers/starten-stoppen/vergunning-nodig/). Cross-check regels-per-gemeente.md: vergunningplicht binds the exploitant, not the client; Amsterdam requires the licence number in every escort ad (art. 3.45 APV).
2. **Agency margin → higher rates.** Agency takes ~30–50% margin on top of what the escort receives, which is why the agency segment starts ~€250/hr where independents start ~€150/hr. Source: escort-prijzen.md (already sourced), consistent figure. Escort agencies are one of the five licensed seksbedrijf forms — [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/vergunning-seksbedrijf/).
3. **What independent means.** KvK-registered (zzp, eenmalige inschrijving €85,15 — woordenboek.md), sets own terms, opts into own screening. In some municipalities solo home-work is licence-free (Utrecht art. 3:4 lid 5 APV, seven conditions; Rotterdam solo without commercial appearance) — cross-check regels-per-gemeente.md / forms explainer.
4. **Trade-offs.** Agency = screening done for you, planning, a real business to complain to (recourse), consistent quality; independent = lower price, direct contact with the person you'll meet, but you do your own vetting.
5. **Verification differs.** With an agency you're trusting the agency's brand + (in Amsterdam) a checkable licence number; with an independent you verify the individual profile yourself (reverse image, tariff check, no advance payment). Link verificatie-uitgelegd + nepprofielen.
6. **Red flags each.** Fake "agency" fronts (a slick site that's really one scammer, no verifiable licence/phone) — no public national register of licensed agencies exists (woordenboek.md, honest weakness). Independents demanding a deposit before you've met = scam (link nepprofielen). Link spot-fakes.

## Opinion (with reason)
For a first-timer or someone booking a hotel outcall in a strange city, an agency's screening and recourse is worth the premium; for a repeat client who knows how to verify a profile, an independent is better value and more direct. The reason: the agency premium buys away the single biggest practical risk (the fake ad), which a novice can't yet check reliably.

## Honest limitation
There's no public national register of licensed escort agencies, so outside Amsterdam (where the ad must carry the licence number) a client usually can't independently confirm an "agency" actually holds a licence — the word "bureau" on a website proves nothing.
