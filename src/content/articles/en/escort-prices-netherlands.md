An escort in the Netherlands typically costs **€150 to €250 per hour** for an independent escort in 2026, rising to **€250 to €400 or more** through an agency or in the high-class segment. Anything advertised well below €100 per hour is almost never real — that is bait pricing or a fake profile.

One caveat on the numbers: no Dutch platform currently publishes a public per-city rate dataset. The figures in this guide are therefore deliberately conservative ranges based on advertised rates across the major Dutch platforms in mid-2026. [Intimate](https://intimate.nl) has announced a live per-city statistics page; once it is online we will cite its data here directly.

## What determines the price of an escort?

Four factors set almost the entire rate: **duration, incall vs outcall, city, and independent vs agency**. Everything else — experience, languages, exclusivity — moves the price within those bands.

- **Duration.** The first hour is the most expensive hour. Two hours rarely costs double: expect roughly 1.7 to 1.8 times the hourly rate. An overnight (8–12 hours) typically runs €800 to €1,200 with independents and €1,500 to €2,500 in the high-class segment.
- **Incall or outcall.** Incall means you visit the escort's private address or workspace — that is the base rate. Outcall means she comes to your home or hotel, adding €30 to €80 for travel time and transport, plus mileage or taxi costs outside her own city. Tourists booking hotel visits in Amsterdam should budget for this on top of the hourly rate.
- **Independent or agency.** An agency handles screening, scheduling and marketing and takes a margin — usually 30 to 50% on top of what the escort receives. That is why the agency segment starts around €250 per hour where independents start near €150. Part of what you pay for is vetting and reliability, not just the appointment.
- **Specialisation.** BDSM, duo bookings, dinner dates and travel companionship carry their own rates, typically 20 to 50% above the standard hourly rate.

Sex work has been legal and regulated in the Netherlands since the brothel ban was lifted in 2000 — escorts pay VAT and income tax, and escort agencies need a licence from their municipality. Those legal operating costs are baked into the rate. For the full legal picture, see [our guide to escort law in the Netherlands](/en/law/is-escort-legal-netherlands/).

## How much does an escort cost per city?

**Amsterdam is by far the most expensive city; Rotterdam, The Hague and Utrecht cluster in the mid-range.** These are realistic one-hour ranges for an independent escort in 2026:

| City | Typical hourly rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Amsterdam | €200 – €350 | Tourist premium, heavy hotel-outcall demand, large high-class segment |
| Utrecht | €150 – €250 | Smaller supply, central location, prices trending toward Amsterdam |
| The Hague (Den Haag) | €150 – €230 | Expat and diplomat city, stable mid-range |
| Rotterdam | €140 – €220 | Largest supply after Amsterdam, sharpest rates of the big four |
| Smaller cities | €120 – €200 | Less choice but lower rates; outcall surcharges weigh heavier |

The Amsterdam premium is mostly a demand story: international visitors book hotel outcalls at a far higher rate than locals, and hotel work carries extra travel time and risk that gets priced in. If you are booking there, our [Amsterdam escort city guide](/en/cities/escort-amsterdam/) covers the local specifics. In Rotterdam and The Hague, supply is deep enough that comparison shopping keeps rates honest.

## What is included in the price?

**The agreed rate covers the agreed time and the services discussed in advance — nothing more, nothing less.** A professional escort is clear upfront about what falls inside the rate. Vagueness on that point is itself a warning sign.

Watch for these pricing constructions:

- **Bait pricing.** An €80-per-hour ad where, on arrival, everything turns out to be "extra" and the real price is €250. Reputable escorts advertise the actual rate.
- **Too cheap is a real red flag.** Below roughly €100 per hour, a legal independent escort in the Netherlands can barely operate: VAT, advertising, workspace and transport do not allow it. In practice, ads at that level are almost always fake profiles fishing for a deposit. Our step-by-step guide to [spotting fake escort profiles and scams](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/) shows how to check before you book.
- **Mid-appointment price hikes.** Demanding more money halfway through for what was already agreed is not negotiation — leave.

Always compare rates across more than one platform; our overview of the [best escort sites in the Netherlands](/en/platforms/best-escort-sites-netherlands/) covers which ones advertise honestly.

## Should you tip an escort in the Netherlands?

**No — tipping is not expected in the Netherlands, and nobody will think less of you for paying exactly the agreed rate.** This surprises American visitors in particular, where tipping is customary in the industry. Dutch service culture treats the quoted price as the full price.

If you want to give something extra after a genuinely good experience, rounding up (€250 instead of €230) or adding around 10% is the usual gesture. Offering a tip in advance to unlock "something extra" lands badly: the agreed framework is the framework. ## How do you pay — and which payment requests are scams?

**Cash at the start of the appointment is the norm in the Netherlands.** You hand over the agreed amount when you arrive — in an envelope or simply counted out — and money is not mentioned again. Some escorts and agencies also accept a Dutch payment link (Tikkie) or card payment on the spot, and high-class agencies sometimes invoice business clients.

The line between normal and scam comes down to one word: **before**.

- **Normal:** paying in person, at the start of the appointment. An established, licensed agency asking for a bank-transfer deposit on a hotel booking can be legitimate — but only from an agency with a licence, a fixed phone number and a verifiable track record.
- **Always a scam:** anyone who wants payment before you have ever met, via **paysafecard, gift cards (Apple, Google Play), crypto, or an anonymous payment link** — usually framed as "verification", "reservation" or "driver costs". These payment methods are irreversible and anonymous; the appointment never happens. This is the single most reported escort scam at the Dutch national fraud desk, [Fraudehelpdesk](https://www.fraudehelpdesk.nl/). One rule to remember: **a real escort never asks for vouchers or gift cards.**

If you have already paid into one of these requests, save every chat and payment record and report it to Fraudehelpdesk and the Dutch police via politie.nl.

## What is a realistic budget for a first booking?

**For a first one-hour booking with an independent escort, budget €150 to €250, plus a €30 to €80 outcall surcharge if she comes to you.** In Amsterdam, that same hour is more likely €200 to €350. Going through an agency starts around €250 per hour, with vetting and mediation included in that price.

Two final practical notes. Booking a longer slot beats hunting for the cheapest hour: the per-hour rate drops and the appointment is more relaxed. And never let price be your only filter — a verified profile at a market-standard rate is always a better choice than the cheapest ad on the page.
