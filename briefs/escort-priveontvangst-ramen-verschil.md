## Researched specifics (all fetch-verified 2026-08-01)
1. **Five licence-requiring sex-business forms** (exploitatievergunning from the gemeente): seksbioscopen, bordelen, escortbedrijven, raamprostitutiebedrijven, erotische massagesalons. Conditions: approved bedrijfsplan, Bibob screening, owners/managers/workers 21+ at licensed businesses. — https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/vergunning-seksbedrijf/
2. **Licence sits with the operator, not the worker.** Window workers and club workers need no licence themselves; the raamexploitant/club owner does. A fully independent escort (own ads, contact, bookings, transport, finances) needs no licence in Amsterdam; mediation by anyone else = licensed escort business. — https://amsterdamcenterforsexworkers.nl/voor-sekswerkers/starten-stoppen/vergunning-nodig/
3. **Amsterdam issues no permits for thuisontvangst** (conflicts with zoning/bestemmingsplan) → structural home reception effectively not possible legally there. — same ACS page
4. **Rotterdam allows solo home work without a licence** under strict conditions: registered at the address, working alone, one workplace, advertising at most once per 13 months ("U adverteert niet vaker dan 1 keer in de 13 maanden"). Anyone advertising weekly therefore counts as a licensable sex business. Rotterdam has 4 licence categories incl. escort = "bemiddeling tussen klant en sekswerker"; 21+ at licensed businesses. — https://www.rotterdam.nl/sekswerk
5. **Window decline numbers**: ±1,500 windows in 2010 → ±1,000 now, spread over fewer than ten cities; Utrecht closed all windows (Zandpad) in 2013; Amsterdam has explored moving Wallen windows to an erotic centre. — NOS, 16 July 2025, https://nos.nl/artikel/2575211
6. **Definitions** (Sekswerkerfgoed, vormen-overzicht): raamprostitutiebedrijf = worker rents a room with window, operator has no say over working method or clients; club = bar/alcohol licence, communal choosing; privéhuis = smaller, no bar; escort = worker travels to client location. — https://sekswerkerfgoed.nl/vormen-van-prostitutie-een-overzicht/
7. **Market taxonomy observation** (in-repo, kinky-review): Kinky.nl lists "escort" and "privéontvangst" as separate ad categories — the market itself splits on who travels.
8. **Amsterdam 21+ at licensed businesses since July 2013** (established in nl/is-escort-legaal.md with sources).
9. **Price bands** (advertised rates mid-2026, conservative; consistent with our prijzen-gids and escort-amsterdam guide): window ±€50 for 15–20 min opening rate; privéontvangst €120–€200/h; independent escort €150–€250/h; agency €250–€400/h; club = entrance + per-time per worker, totals near escort level.

## Opinion (with reason)
For most readers, an escort or privéontvangst booked via a verified profile is the best fit: you control time, place and pace, and verification removes the biggest practical risk (fake ads). Escort for hotel stays/discretion; privéontvangst for budget with no venue of your own; windows for no-appointment transparency; clubs for choosing in person.

## Flaws to state
- Escort: most expensive form; open platforms carry the highest fake-profile risk.
- Privéontvangst: you are a guest at an unknown address; in Amsterdam formally unlicensed (no oversight).
- Windows: short and transactional; De Wallen = tourist crowds.
- Clubs: you pay for the venue and bar on top.
