## What is Hunqz and who runs it?

Hunqz is the escort section of ROMEO, the gay social network most people still know as PlanetRomeo. Per Wikipedia, "sex workers and escorts can also advertise their services by creating their own profiles on the Hunqz section of the website." So this is not a standalone escort startup — it is the paid-companionship arm bolted onto one of the oldest gay platforms in Europe.

The operator is the interesting part for a Dutch reader. ROMEO B.V. runs it from Amsterdam, and has done since September 2006; the platform itself started in Berlin in October 2002 as GayRomeo, became PlanetRomeo in 2011, and simplified to ROMEO in 2021. Twenty-plus years of gay-platform history, headquartered in the Netherlands.

That heritage explains both the strength and the gap. Hunqz inherits a large, international, multilingual gay audience — the reason its inventory and language reach beat every rival in our sweep. It also inherits a community-social model where trust was always peer-to-peer, which is exactly why its verification is so thin.

Our opinion, with the reason attached: Hunqz gives gay clients in the Netherlands the reach and the Dutch-language access nobody else does, and then leaves the single most important safety feature to other users. Reach without checks is the same trade the general Dutch market makes — it is just more visible here because the inventory is so much bigger than the alternatives.

## How good is the verification?

A 3, and the low score is the honest one. There is no document check and no ID check before a profile goes live. As a long-standing review of the ROMEO platform puts it, "some profiles are verified, but only by other users — if you know one of the members in person, you can mark that on their profile."

Read that carefully. A verified mark on Hunqz tells you that somebody claims to know this person, not that the platform confirmed who they are. That is weaker than an optional official badge and far weaker than the mandatory photo checks we have seen elsewhere. Our [guide to verification](/en/guides/escort-verification-explained/) lays out what each type of check actually proves — peer vouching proves the least.

For comparison, this is the opposite end of the scale from a platform that checks every advertiser before publication. Hunqz checks none of them.

## How is the mobile experience?

A 7. Hunqz rides ROMEO's infrastructure, which includes real native apps for Android and iOS, free to download, alongside the mobile web. For a sector where most sites are a desktop layout squeezed onto a phone, having an actual maintained app is a genuine edge. The listings, ratings and preference fields all work one-handed. It is not the most modern-feeling product in the market, but it is well ahead of the dated Dutch options.

## How fast is the site?

A 6. Pages are image-heavy and the underlying platform is old, so nothing feels snappy, and Hunqz sits behind a bot wall — our automated tools were blocked outright while a normal browser loaded the site fine. If it ever seems "down" for you, a plain browser usually gets through. Usable, not fast.

## How transparent is pricing?

A 5. On the plus side, escorts' own rates appear on their profiles — hourly and overnight figures, alongside ratings and service details, per the ROMEO/Hunqz product description. That is more than some directories manage.

The deduction is the platform side. What it costs to advertise, and which paid features sit behind which tier, is not laid out plainly the way the best platforms publish it. You learn the cost by using the site, and there is no single clean price page for a client to check before diving in. Rates you do see should be confirmed by message before any booking.

## How good is the moderation?

A 4. There are signals in the right direction: a support channel exists for "bullying, fake accounts, or any other problem," and Hunqz maintains a public presence on review sites. But there is no published pre-publication review, fake-account reports are a recurring theme, and with peer-only verification the moderation has very little to work with. A platform that does not check identities up front is always moderating after the fact.

A 7 — its best score alongside inventory, and no accident. That makes Hunqz the rare gay-escort site a Dutch speaker can use in their own language, and an easy landing for English-speaking visitors too. In a market where most adult platforms are Dutch-only or English-only, working in both is a real advantage.
