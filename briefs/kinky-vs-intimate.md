## Opinion (stated with reason)
The two platforms are mirror images by structural choice, not by accident: Kinky's open, optional-verification model buys the 10 for inventory and causes the 6s on trust; Intimate's mandatory verification buys the 10 on trust and causes the 4 on inventory. Which one is "best" is therefore a question about the reader, not the platforms.

## Flaw (both sides, mandatory)
- Kinky: no default verification; documented fraud cases (2022 Pointer, 2025 arrests); dated, slow site.
- Intimate: early access, small inventory (in most towns a handful of profiles), no multi-year track record.
