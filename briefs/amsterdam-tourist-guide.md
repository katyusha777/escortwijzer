## Researched specifics (fetch-verified 2026-08-01, or from our own published guides)

1. **Legality for the visitor:** sex work between consenting adults is legal nationwide (government.nl/topics/prostitution); Amsterdam licenses escort agencies (APV art. 3.40) and bans street solicitation (art. 2.12). Nothing about being a foreign client changes any of this.
   Sources: https://www.government.nl/topics/prostitution · https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR72510/36
2. **English availability:** high — platforms and independents advertise in EN as standard; no "translator"/middleman is ever needed (established in our Amsterdam guide).
3. **Money (keep consistent with /en/guides/escort-prices-netherlands/):** Amsterdam independents €200–€350/hr; outcall surcharge €30–€80; agencies from ~€250/hr; overnight €800–€1,200 (independents); windows from ~€50 for 15–20 min. Cash at the start of the appointment is the norm; some accept Tikkie/card on the spot. Any pre-meeting payment by voucher/gift card/crypto = scam, the single most reported escort scam at Fraudehelpdesk.
   Source: https://www.fraudehelpdesk.nl/ + our price guide.
4. **Hotels:** most Amsterdam hotels allow registered guests' visitors; some hostels/budget places require visitor registration or ban non-guests after a set hour; policies vary per property and are not reliably published — advise checking house rules at booking. No naming of chains (unverifiable).
5. **Timing:** evenings and weekends are peak; same-night booking possible but thin on choice; 24–48h advance books the calmer, better end of the market. (Observation/opinion, flagged as such — no dataset exists.)
6. **Scams aimed at tourists:** taxi-driver "recommendation" (commission clip joints), street offers (illegal per APV 2.12), deposit/voucher ads, too-cheap pricing. All covered with sources in existing pieces; cross-link spot-fakes.
7. **Windows ≠ escorts:** two-sentence explainer, link the Amsterdam city guide for De Wallen detail.

## The opinion
Booking 24–48 hours ahead from home beats landing-and-scrolling: the visible Friday-night supply is the market's thinnest, most scam-ridden slice, and the professionals worth seeing are often booked out. Patience is the cheapest verification tool there is.

## The flaw (in what we recommend)
Platform booking as the recommended route: verification standards differ per platform and no platform catches every fake; and for a tourist the cash-first norm means carrying a few hundred euros, which is its own small risk.
