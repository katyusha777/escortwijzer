## Opinion (with reason)
Platforms that use respectful category language AND verify advertisers serve trans clients
and workers better than the big directories do — because for a group that faces more fakes,
outing risk and violence, the terminology on the filter and the check behind the badge are
the same signal: whether the platform treats trans people as people or as a search tag.

## Flaw (honest limitation)
Every route has a cost. The platforms with the most trans listings (EuroGirlsEscort, the
Shemale4u-style sites) also carry the slurring taxonomy and voluntary/for-sale
verification; the respectful, verified option (Intimate) has a small trans catalogue.
There is no large, verified, respectfully-labelled trans platform in NL today.
