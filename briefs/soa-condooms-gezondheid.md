## Researched specifics (fetch-verified 2026-08-01)

1. **Condom norm in Dutch sex work:** condoms are standard and expected in the Dutch sex industry — the default, not a negotiable extra. A professional who insists on a condom is behaving normally; someone (client or worker) pushing to skip it is the red flag.
   Source: https://www.soaaids.nl/nl/fijne-gezonde-seks/condooms
2. **Condoms reduce but don't eliminate risk:** soaaids.nl states condoms make an STI less likely ("Heb je seks met een condoom, dan krijg je minder snel een soa") but do NOT fully protect against all STIs — some (herpes, genital warts/HPV) spread via skin not covered by the condom. This is the honest limitation.
   Source: https://www.soaaids.nl/nl/fijne-gezonde-seks/condooms
3. **"No condom" ad claim as a warning sign:** an ad advertising condomless service (bareback/"zonder condoom") signals someone who takes that risk with every client — a health red flag and, given the norm, a professionalism/legitimacy red flag. (Opinion, reasoned.)
4. **STI/soa testing in NL — where:** GP (huisarts) or a GGD Soa-poli / STI outpatient clinic; home tests exist and are as reliable as GP/GGD tests per soaaids.nl.
   Source: https://www.soaaids.nl/nl/soa-test/afspraak-bij-huisarts-of-ggd + https://www.soaaids.nl/nl/soa-test/soa-thuistest
5. **GGD free/anonymous testing — who:** GGD testing is free and can be anonymous for higher-risk / higher-threshold groups, incl. persons under 25, sex workers, men who have sex with men, people with symptoms or a partner with an STI. Not everyone qualifies for free GGD testing; others go via the GP (billed to insurance / eigen risico).
   Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/gezondheid-en-preventie/seksuele-gezondheid/seksuele-gezondheidszorg
6. **Sense for under-25s:** under-25s can use Sense (sense.info, run by the GGD) for free, confidential advice and testing; GP/insurer/parents are not informed unless you ask.
   Source: https://sense.info/nl + rijksoverheid page above
7. **PrEP context:** since 2024 free PrEP CARE is available via the GGD for people at elevated HIV risk (broadened beyond MSM/trans), but the PILLS are paid by the user (~€30 per 30 tablets, varies per pharmacy); many GGDs have waiting lists. PrEP prevents HIV, NOT other STIs — condoms still matter.
   Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/gezondheid-en-preventie/seksuele-gezondheid/prep-zorg-en-pillen
8. **What safer-sex boundaries signal:** a professional with clear, non-negotiable safer-sex boundaries is showing they do this properly and repeatedly — a legitimacy signal, not a downside. (Opinion, reasoned.)

## The opinion
A professional who insists on condoms is a legitimacy signal, not a downside. Consistent safer-sex boundaries mean someone who works to a standard and expects to do it again next week — the opposite of a red flag.

## The flaw (honest limitation)
Condoms do not eliminate all STI risk — herpes and HPV can transmit via uncovered skin (soaaids.nl). Safer sex lowers risk; it does not zero it. And we cannot give medical advice: for anything specific, see the GP/GGD/soaaids.nl, not this page.
