## Answer (2 sentences)
Escort is legal in Maastricht via licensed businesses under Hoofdstuk 3 of the APV Maastricht (CVDR676542, in force 1 Jan 2024); running a sex establishment or escort business without a permit is prohibited (art. 3.4). Maastricht has no red-light district and no window prostitution — the whole market runs by appointment.

## Researched specifics (WITH sources — fetch-verified 2026-08-01)
- **APV = CVDR676542**, "Algemene plaatselijke verordening Maastricht", in force since 1 Jan 2024 (replaced APV 2006). https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR676542/2
- **Hoofdstuk 3** = "Seksinrichtingen, sekswinkels, straatprostitutie e.d."
- **Art. 3.1** — definitions incl. *escortbedrijf* (prostitution offered commercially, carried out somewhere other than the business premises). https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR676542/2
- **Art. 3.4(1)** — vergunningplicht: "verboden een seksinrichting of een escortbedrijf te exploiteren ... zonder vergunning." https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR676542/2
- **Art. 3.5** — operator/manager (exploitant/beheerder) must be **21+**. NOT a rule for the sex workers themselves.
- **Art. 3.10** — "Straatprostitutie – raamprostitutie" (window/street prostitution regulated/prohibited; full verbatim not captured — cite article number only).
- **Age of sex workers:** APV sets NO explicit 18/21 minimum for the workers. National baseline is 18+ (Dutch law). FLAG openly — do not claim a local 21 for workers.
- **No window prostitution / no red-light district** in Maastricht — no permit for a window prostitution business; Maastricht not listed by heritage/wiki sources as a window city. https://sekswerkerfgoed.nl/omvang-raamprostitutie/ · https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raamprostitutie
- **Enforcement:** illegal prostitution found ~20 times 2018–2023 (gemeente press release; body 403 — cite as "per gemeente" cautiously or omit specifics).
- **Cross-border:** Maastricht → Aachen (DE) ~30 km road; Maastricht → Liège/Luik (BE) ~30 km road. https://www.travelmath.com/drive-distance/from/Maastricht,+Netherlands/to/Aachen,+Germany
- **Price band:** DERIVED, not measured. Regional city, no tourist premium → ~€130–€190/hr independent, like Eindhoven. Flag as derived. National frame: /gidsen/escort-prijzen. Outcall +€30–80.

## Opinion (with reason)
The tri-border position makes Maastricht's online-only market a magnet for out-of-region fake ads (a German/Belgian visitor can't check a physical location), so verification matters more here than in a window city — book only via verified profiles.

## Honest limitation
Gemeente publishes no public register of licensed escort businesses, so a client cannot independently verify a Maastricht permit. Also: the APV does not publish a worker age rule, so we state the national 18 floor, not a local number.
