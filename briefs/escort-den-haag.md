## Researched specifics (all fetch-verified 2026-08-01)

1. **Licensing.** Exploitatievergunning seksbedrijf (includes escortbureaus): granted for 1 year at a time, leges €1.704 (new permit; €373,90 for a manager change), Bibob check every 4 years, sekswerkers must be 21+ and KVK-registered. Consistent with nl/is-escort-legaal.md. Source: denhaag.nl/nl/vergunningen-en-ontheffingen/exploitatievergunning-seksbedrijf-aanvragen/.
2. **Maximumstelsel since 1 Oct 1999** — number of seksinrichtingen per location fixed; new businesses only via takeover (already used in is-escort-legaal.md, same source).
3. **Windows EXIST.** Two licensed window areas: Doubletstraat (~190 windows, Oude Centrum) and Geleenstraat + adjacent Hunsestraat (~140 windows). Third street Poeldijksestraat (300 windows) closed 2005, demolished 2006. Source: nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostitutie_in_Den_Haag.
4. **Doubletstraat = "goedkoopste prostitutiestraat van Nederland"**, rates around €30 per visit cited. Source: Omroep West 20-02-2020 (3999248).
5. **Closure debate — actual 2026 state: OPEN.** Timeline: relocation plans (Sporendriehoek near CS — not feasible; later erotic centre near Schenkviaduct/Binckhorst) failed. Council ran three pilots (hourly room rental — zero users; shared work location — not feasible; digital client contact — rejected by workers). 11 Oct 2025: council votes 23–20 to keep windows open and continue via pilots/consultation; CDA+DENK motion demands scenarios for phased ending by April 2026. May 2026 evaluation: no pilot produced a full alternative; closure would cost tens of millions (expropriation/compensation); dossier passed to the coalition formed after the March 2026 elections. Windows open as of Aug 2026. Sources: omroepwest.nl/politiek/5021609, omroepwest.nl/politiek/5097600, dagblad070.nl (07-05-2026).
6. **Prices.** Our price guide: Den Haag €150–€230/hr independent escort — stable mid-segment, expat/diplomat city. Source: /nl/gidsen/escort-prijzen/.
7. **SHOP** (Stichting Hulp en Opvang Prostitutie en Mensenhandel) is the named support org (already in is-escort-legaal.md, denhaag.nl).

## Opinion (with reason)
Keeping the windows open was the right call: three pilots produced zero working alternatives (the hourly-rental pilot attracted literally no sex workers), and closing 330 windows with nothing to replace them moves the work to unlicensed apartments — less safe for workers and clients alike. The council's own evaluation says as much.

## Flaw / honest limitation
Den Haag publishes its permit fee but no register of licensed escort agencies, so clients cannot independently check a vergunning; and window prices tell you nothing about escort prices — the €30 Doubletstraat figure is a different product in a different market.
