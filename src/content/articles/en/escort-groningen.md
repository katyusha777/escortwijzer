Hiring an escort in Groningen is legal: sex work between consenting adults is permitted throughout the Netherlands, and Groningen licenses escort businesses under article 3:3 of its municipal by-law, the APVG. What sets Groningen apart is a rule no other major city applies so strictly: every sex worker in a licensed business must first sit a personal intake interview with the municipality.

The city has one window street, the Nieuwstad, and beyond it the market runs by appointment — escorts to your hotel or apartment, and independents receiving at private addresses. This guide covers what the city requires, how the Nieuwstad works for a visitor, and what a booking realistically costs.

## Is escort legal in Groningen?

Yes, under municipal licensing for businesses and an extra requirement on the workers. The Netherlands legalised the operation of sex businesses in 2000, and article 3:3 of the APVG prohibits running a sex business without a permit. An escort permit is valid for one year at a time and tied to a specific premises. An agency mediating without one is breaking the law — the client booking through it generally is not, but you'd be funding the illegal end of a legal market.

Three rules worth knowing:

- **Minimum age 21.** Licensed Groningen businesses may only work with sex workers aged 21 or over — stricter than the national minimum of 18.
- **Mandatory intake interview.** Before working in a licensed business, the municipality holds a personal intake interview (*intakegesprek*, art. 3:13–3:14 APVG). The intake certificate is valid for 24 months and is refused to anyone under 21 or without registration at the Chamber of Commerce (*KvK*) and the population register (*BRP*). Without it, an operator may not let you work.
- **Few escort agencies.** The mayor grants an exemption for at most five escort businesses. Groningen deliberately keeps the licensed escort circuit small.

That intake interview is the city's most distinctive rule. For the national legal picture — what is actually criminal, how age and coercion rules work — see our explainer on [whether escorts are legal in the Netherlands](/en/law/is-escort-legal-netherlands/).

## What is the Nieuwstad?

The Nieuwstad is Groningen's only window location. Since 2005 the street has held about 40 prostitution buildings with 95 windows in a near-continuous gallery on both sides, over roughly 300 metres, and since 2016 the city concentrates all window prostitution here — there are no other window locations left.

For a visitor that makes the Nieuwstad a real, contiguous window street — smaller than Amsterdam's De Wallen, but clearly recognisable as one. You see who's working on the spot, but you arrange nothing in advance and screen no one: what you see on the street is what there is. The city publishes no figure for how many windows are occupied at any given time, so we won't invent one.

## How is the rest of the scene organised?

Away from the Nieuwstad, everything runs by appointment, in two formats — and both work in English, as the Dutch escort scene does every day.

**Outcall escort.** The escort comes to your hotel or apartment. Booked through a licensed agency (there are at most five) or directly with an independent on an advertising platform.

**Incall / privéontvangst.** You go to the escort — a working apartment or their own place. *Privéontvangst* means "private reception" at the worker's own address, usually the cheapest option because no agency margin or travel time is priced in. The differences between escorts, private reception and windows are laid out in our [guide to the Dutch formats](/en/law/escort-brothel-windows-difference/).

Groningen is a student city — the largest in the north, with a young population around its university and college — and the demand reflects that: relatively high, young, local. The online supply is lively as a result, which is also the weakness: every fake profile can claim to be in Groningen, and there's no physical place to verify who actually works there. ## How much does an escort cost in Groningen?

Expect roughly €120 to €170 per hour for an independent escort. The northern Netherlands typically sits below the national average — lower cost of living, and none of the tourist premium that lifts Amsterdam rates. Agencies charge more, and a hotel outcall adds a €30 to €80 travel surcharge, standard nationwide.

Honest caveat on that figure: we have no live Groningen dataset. This band is derived from the national framework in our [Dutch escort price guide](/en/guides/escort-prices-netherlands/), adjusted down for the lower northern price level — not from locally measured numbers. Treat it as a guide, not a quote.

One number holds everywhere, here included: below €100 per hour, an ad is almost never real. Dutch taxes, advertising and workspace costs make legal work at that rate impossible, so a bargain-priced "Groningen" profile is bait pricing or a deposit scam.

## Can an escort visit my hotel in Groningen?

Almost always. Hotel outcalls are the standard booking, and most Groningen hotels allow registered guests to receive visitors. Two practical points: budget hotels and hostels sometimes require visitors to register at reception, and expect screening in both directions — a professional will ask your hotel name, room number and the name on the booking. That's not suspicion, it's standard practice, and a better sign than someone who asks nothing at all.

Payment is cash at the start of the appointment, in euros; some escorts take a card or Dutch payment app on the spot. What nobody legitimate does is demand prepayment by payment link, gift card or crypto before you've met — that is the single most common escort scam aimed at foreigners, and our guide to [spotting fake escort profiles](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/) shows how to filter it out.

## How do you book safely in Groningen?

Through a verified profile on a serious platform, one of the five licensed agencies, or in person at the Nieuwstad. Booking online, the basics hold: check the profile for verified photos and a consistent ad history (our [spot-fakes guide](/en/guides/spot-fake-escort-profiles/) runs through every check), and never pay in advance remotely — cash at the start is the norm.

Our view: the mandatory intake interview is the most sex-worker-friendly rule of the cities we compare. The municipality meets every worker in the licensed circuit once, in person, which surfaces coerced or illegal situations earlier than a paper-only permit does — and the KvK and BRP requirement on top makes anonymous exploitation within that circuit harder still. The honest caveat: it says nothing about the unlicensed online market, which, as everywhere, is where most of the supply sits.

And the broader limitation every Dutch city shares: the municipality publishes no register of licensed escort agencies.
