## Answer (2 sentences)
Escort is legal in Arnhem via licensed businesses under Hoofdstuk 3 of the APV (CVDR418922); operating a sex establishment or escort business needs a municipal permit (art. 3.2.1). The famous Spijkerkwartier window district closed on 4 January 2006 and Arnhem has had no operating window prostitution since — the market is by appointment only.

## Researched specifics (WITH sources — fetch-verified 2026-08-01)
- **APV = CVDR418922**, "Algemene Plaatselijke Verordening voor Arnhem". https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR418922
- **Hoofdstuk 3** = "Seksinrichtingen, sekswinkels, straatprostitutie e.d."
- **Art. 3.1.1** — definitions (seksbedrijf / escortbedrijf); verbatim Arnhem text truncated in renderer — cite article number, generic Dutch-model meaning.
- **Art. 3.2.1** — "Vergunning": licensing requirement for seksinrichtingen. https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR418922/7
- **Age:** operators/managers must be **21+** (beleidsregel 2024, CVDR723574: "exploitanten en beheerder ... minimaal 21 jaar"). Worker minimum age NOT stated in Arnhem text (minors prohibited only). National baseline 18+. FLAG — no local worker age number.
- **Spijkerkwartier:** window prostitution from 1965, 2nd-largest raam district in NL after De Wallen; **closed 4 January 2006** (after Raad van State 23 Nov 2005). https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostitutie_in_Arnhem · https://www.gld.nl/nieuws/2104091/spijkerkwartier-arnhem-van-hoeren-naar-hip-en-happening
- **Replacement:** a zoned alternative on the Westervoortsedijk (Billiton-terrein, up to 120 windows) was designated but **no Eroscentrum was ever built; zero window workplaces / zero exploitanten as of 2024** (down from ~19 exploitanten / ~240 windows in 2000). "Eroscentrum komt er nooit." https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR723574/1 · https://www.arnhem-direct.nl/berichten/beloofd-eroscentrum-komter-nooit/
- **Licensed establishments:** 3 licensed seksinrichtingen (June 2024; max 8). Beleidsregel CVDR723574. https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR723574
- **Home work (thuiswerk): NOT allowed** — "prostitutie vanuit huis is op dit moment niet toegestaan" (conflicts with omgevingsplan); estimated ~2,200 sex workers pushed into illegality; 2024 policy under revision to bring sex work out of illegality. https://www.gld.nl/nieuws/8148800/arnhem-wil-sekswerk-uit-de-illegaliteit-halen-levert-veel-risicos-op
- **Border:** ~15 km to the German border line (east/southeast toward Zevenaar/Elten); nearest German town Emmerich am Rhein ~31 km by road. https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Arnhem/Emmerich

## Opinion (with reason)
Arnhem's home-work ban is a policy misfire: barring privéontvangst doesn't remove demand, it just moves an estimated ~2,200 workers into illegality where neither client screening nor worker safety improves — the 2024 review to end that is overdue.

## Honest limitation
No public register of licensed escort businesses — a client can't independently verify an Arnhem permit. And the APV publishes no worker age number, so we cite the national 18 floor.
