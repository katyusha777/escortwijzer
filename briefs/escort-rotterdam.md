## Researched specifics (all fetch-verified 2026-08-01)

1. **Licensing.** Rotterdam regulates sex businesses in hoofdstuk 3 of the APV Rotterdam 2012 (CVDR373493, lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl). Four permit categories; the escortbedrijf is defined as "bemiddeling tussen klant en sekswerker". Source: rotterdam.nl/sekswerk + lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR373493.
2. **Minimum age 21.** Licensed businesses may only let sekswerkers of 21+ work; violation risks the permit. Source: rotterdam.nl/sekswerk.
3. **Home-work exemption.** Self-employed sekswerkers may work from their own home WITHOUT a permit if they work alone, are registered (BRP) at that address, and use a single workspace. Source: rotterdam.nl/sekswerk.
4. **No windows.** Rotterdam phased out raamprostitutie from the late 1970s; the Katendrecht window scene was gone by the early 1990s. Sources: nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostitutie_in_Rotterdam, sekswerkerfgoed.nl.
5. **Keileweg tippelzone closed 2005.** Opened 1 Nov 1994, closed September 2005 (Leefbaar-college decision 2004); no legal street zone since. Sources: sekswerkerfgoed.nl/de-werkplek-de-tippelzone-aan-de-keileweg-1994-2005/, nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keileweg.
6. **Support orgs.** Door2Door (info centre), Humanitas meldpunt uitbuiting 010 236 52 56, Stichting de Haven. Source: rotterdam.nl/sekswerk.
7. **Prices.** Our own price guide: Rotterdam €140–€220/hr independent escort — sharpest rates of the G4, largest supply after Amsterdam. Outcall surcharge €30–€80 national norm. Source: /nl/gidsen/escort-prijzen/.

## Opinion (with reason)
Rotterdam's setup — no window zone, no street zone, escort + privéontvangst with a solo home-work exemption — is the most sensible client market of the G4: no tourist circus means fewer scams aimed at outsiders and the sharpest prices, and the home-work exemption keeps solo workers inside the legal system instead of pushing them under it.

## Flaw / honest limitation
There is no public register of licensed Rotterdam escort agencies, so a client cannot independently verify a vergunning; and because the scene is invisible (no windows), the online ad market is the whole market — which is exactly where fake profiles live.
