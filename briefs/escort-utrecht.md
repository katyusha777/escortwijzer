## Researched specifics (all fetch-verified 2026-08-01)

1. **Old Zandpad:** 34 boats with 142 workspaces on the Vecht (1999 municipal count); window prostitution there since 1945, official zone since the 1973 APV change. Closed 25 July 2013 — municipality withdrew the operators' licences over human-trafficking signals; ended ~70 years of prostitution on the Vecht.
   Source: https://sekswerkerfgoed.nl/geschiedenis-van-het-utrechtse-zandpad/
2. **Raad van State upheld the withdrawal** (2016): the municipality was entitled to revoke the boat operators' licences.
   Source: https://www.raadvanstate.nl/@8904/utrecht-mocht/
3. **Nieuwe Zandpad saga:** council approved a purpose-built replacement in Sept 2015 (162 windows, court-capped at 145 in 2016); investors dropped out in 2017; a foundation was selected to build 96 workspaces, but on 5 Nov 2021 the city scrapped the plan definitively — the foundation could not get financing (its bank demanded municipal guarantees; the council refused, wanting to be landlord only).
   Source: https://nos.nl/artikel/2404508-toch-streep-door-nieuwe-plek-voor-raamprostituees-in-utrecht
4. **2026 status — the pivot to small-scale:** after the Sorgdrager commission's advice (small-scale, spread across the city), Utrecht now runs two pilots: (a) **thuiswerk legal since 1 Jan 2025** — sex workers may work from their own home; (b) a small licensed business of **max 15 workspaces on the 2nd floor of parking garage Paardenveld** (designed to be invisible from outside; council decision June 2025; maintenance from April 2026; news on final realisation expected after summer 2026). There is currently **no window prostitution and no tippelzone in Utrecht** — gemeente's own words.
   Sources: https://www.utrecht.nl/ondernemen/sekswerk · https://www.utrecht.nl/wonen-en-leven/bouwprojecten-en-stedelijke-ontwikkeling/bouwprojecten/binnenstad/paardenveld-tijdelijke-invulling-parkeergarage
5. **Licensing today:** escort businesses need a municipal licence (APV Utrecht 2010, art. 3:4 — verboden zonder vergunning; escort businesses must be bound to a fixed address); minimum age for sex workers is 21 (APV art. 3:15 lid 1 sub b). Licensed inventory per the April 2025 enforcement policy: 2 sex businesses + 1 sex cinema; a zoning maximum of 6. Unlicensed rented-property work is an enforcement priority; ≥3 unannounced inspections/year for licensed businesses.
   Sources: https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR318782 (APV) · https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR738007 (Beleidsregel Handhavingsstrategie Sekswerk, 15 Apr 2025)
6. **Hardebollenstraat** (city-centre windows, 17 windows): closed 2013; city bought the properties in May 2016 for €5.8M to keep sex work out. (Wikipedia/DUIC corroborated — use cautiously, secondary.)
7. **Prices:** Utrecht €150–€250/hr independent (national norm band), Amsterdam €200–€350 — from our own price guide; keep numbers identical.

## The opinion
Utrecht is the clearest illustration of the licensing paradox: the city closed 142 legal workplaces in one stroke in 2013 and has managed to replace them, thirteen years on, with a home-work pilot and 15 promised rooms in a parking garage. Well-intentioned enforcement shrank the legal market; demand did not shrink with it — escort and thuiswerk absorbed it. The 2025 thuiswerk rule is genuine progress, though.

## The flaw (in what we recommend)
Escort/platform booking in Utrecht: supply is thin compared with Amsterdam or Rotterdam — fewer profiles, faster-booked evenings, and Utrecht has no public register of licensed escort businesses, so verifying a bureau still partly relies on its own word.
